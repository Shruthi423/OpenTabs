Job Description:
Location: San Francisco, California (Partial Remote)
Product Education helps people understand what a solution can do and how to get the most out of it through various learning content. As the library of educational video content grows, a designer is needed to give that content a consistent, polished visual identity and help make complex ideas easy to follow. This role is a good fit for someone who enjoys visualizing abstract ideas and wants to build the design foundation that a growing content function will rely on for years.
Design visual assets for educational videos, including diagrams, motion graphics, animations, and on-screen text treatments that clarify complex or technical topics.
Partner with producers, writers, and subject matter experts to turn technical concepts into visuals that are accurate and easy to understand.
Develop and maintain a visual language and design system for product education content, spanning video, in-product guidance, and written materials.
Build and maintain design systems, and create reusable templates, motion presets, and components that let the broader team produce consistent content efficiently.
Design storyboards and visual elements for educational videos, working closely with motion designers.
Collaborate with video producers and editors to integrate designed assets into finished videos.
Design thumbnails across the content library to be consistent and inviting.
Design badges and other visual markers that recognize what someone has learned.
Adapt educational content into social-ready assets.
Review and refine designs based on feedback from stakeholders and content performance.
Identify opportunities to improve the visual quality or clarity of existing content.
Stay current on motion design, information design, and educational content trends relevant to technical audiences.
Required Qualifications
Strong design systems experience and a portfolio that includes explainer videos, motion graphics, or other work that makes complex ideas visually clear.
Experience building or contributing to a visual design system or style guide.
Skilled with design and motion tools such as Figma, After Effects, and Illustrator.
Ability to take a technical or abstract concept and find an intuitive visual metaphor or structure.
Strong eye for typography, color, layout, and motion.
Comfortable designing across formats—video, thumbnails, web, and social.
Ability to collaborate effectively with producers, writers, engineers, and motion designers.
Comfortable managing several video projects at once, often with fast turnaround times.
Self-directed and proactive in improving content and systems.
Excited about making AI concepts accessible and understandable to a broad audience.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience in edtech, instructional design, or curriculum development.
Background specifically in motion graphics or animation for educational content.
Experience partnering closely with subject matter experts to distill technical material.
Familiarity with AI or machine learning concepts.
Experience extending or adapting an established design system into a new content area.
