This position offers excellent long-term career progression, with the opportunity to move into Product Development leadership as the business continues to grow.

Design products that meet both technical and commercial objectives.
Produce manufacturing drawing packages and engineering documentation.

Assist with technical aspects of quotation activities.

Minimum of 5 years' experience in a product design or engineering role.
Experience producing detailed engineering drawings.
Comfortable working in a customer-facing, commercially focused environment.
Strong communication and relationship-building skills.
Ability to manage multiple bespoke design projects simultaneously.

Understanding how different metal alloys influence mould and product design.
