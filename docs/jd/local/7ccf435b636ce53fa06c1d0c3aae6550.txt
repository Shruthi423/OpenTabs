Ensure internal web and mobile user-interface design consistency across the organization by collaborating with marketers and other designers, documenting best practices, and organizing design files and working folders to promote team efficiency and synergy.
Maintain current knowledge on industry and design trends (with a focus on user-interface design) to keep the team at the industry’s forefront in content and design spaces.
Required Qualifications
BA/BS Degree (4-year) in Art, Design or related field
2-4 years of digital creative design experience (e.g., in UX/UI)
Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite, including Photoshop, InDesign and Illustrator and Microsoft Office
Strong understanding of web optimization and digital file types
Experience creating digital collateral (i.e., Websites, mobile apps, banners, social media assets, outdoor, etc.)
Strong communication and organizational skills and proven ability to communicate effectively with nondesigners on projects
Must be organized, detail-oriented and able to multi-task and work in a fast-paced high-pressure environment
Self-starter, able to think creatively to solve problems
Strong knowledge and interest in design and demonstrated ability to think creatively
Commitment to continuous learning and personal development
