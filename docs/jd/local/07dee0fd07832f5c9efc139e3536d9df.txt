Moon is a global enterprise accelerator for Mitsui & Co. Our vision is to accelerate Mitsui & Co. through emerging global technologies to create brighter futures everywhere. Our global, multidisciplinary team connects the right people, technologies, and opportunities to deliver impact at scale.
What You’ll Do At Moon
Work closely with the Project Manager to define and execute research scope across new initiatives stages, for heavy industry-focused partnerships and collaborations.
Assist with/execute various aspects of research planning to address project goals, and business/product decisions.
Plan and execute desktop and market research, including but not limited to: trend analysis, competitive landscape, steeple/pestle analysis, etc.
Plan and run qualitative research with key stakeholders, and subject matter experts to surface real operational, safety and commercial unmet needs.
Synthesize research and findings, identify key insights, and collaborate with the team to define implications for key project decisions.
Plan and run concept testing with key stakeholders, including validating emerging concepts against user/stakeholder needs and identifying industry constraints.
Join brainstorming sessions as needed, and be a thought partner to the team - expanding your thinking into different areas but bringing your unique POV/experience to the table (as both a researcher and beyond).
Assist in defining innovation opportunities based on the research for potential projects and working with the team to prioritize what can/should be done.
7+ years of experience with Design Research or UX Research background, with demonstrated qualitative methods.
A degree with course work relevant to Design, the Scientific Process/Methodology, the Human Condition (e.g. Product/Service Design, Psychology, Sociology, etc) OR relevant experience in Design Research, Strategic Design, or UX Research.
Ability to synthesize complex findings into clear, decision ready outputs.
Proactive, self-driven, comfortable working closely with a Project Manager in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment.
Proficiency in Google tools, Slack, Notion, Figma.
Experiences in heavy industries (mining, energy, manufacturing, supply chain or similar) - domain knowledge is a plus but not a requirement.
Previous experience with Exploratory Research in early stage product development/new business creation.
Experience in proof of concept prototyping and iteration.
Experience with Quantitative Research Methodologies / Synthesis.
Experience experimenting AI tools effectively to conduct research.
Experience working with a cross-cultural/international organization.
Experience in/understanding of other design areas (e.g. Solution/Product Design, Service Design, Brand Design, etc.).
Business level Brazilian Portuguese and/or Spanish.
