The Advertiser UX (AUX) team serves as the strategic design center for Amazon Ads, designing end-to-end experiences for advertisers, publishers, partners, and internal account teams across the Ads Console, PubTech, and Sales organizations. Our vision is to enable advertisers of all sizes to effortlessly harness advertising potential through intuitive, intelligent, and unified experiences. Operating across multiple product teams, AUX carries a view of our advertisers that no single domain holds, enabling us to look around corners, inform the creation of bar-raising experiences, and connect the quality of those experiences to the real value they deliver.
Basic Qualifications
3+ years of front-end technologist, engineer, or UX prototyper experience
Have coding samples in front end programming languages
Experience with UI design for complex workflows and for end users
BS degree in computer science or equivalent, or 4+ years of technical work experience
Have an online portfolio or samples of work on resume, demonstrating experience creating great end-to-end, user-centered design solutions and patterns, across desktop and mobile devices
Experience with web, mobile web, and mobile app development including both front-end and back-end (e.g., building APIs, services, or cloud-based applications)
Proficiency with Figma and modern front-end frameworks (React, TypeScript)
Experience building applications that integrate LLMs, AI/ML APIs, or agentic workflows
Preferred Qualifications
Uses a metrics-driven approach to building, testing, and iterating on AI agents, complex skills/steering files, or MCP servers
Takes advantage of the latest agentic UX patterns and invents new ones when necessary
Uses emerging AI tools and techniques to create solutions that were previously impossible or unfeasible to implement
