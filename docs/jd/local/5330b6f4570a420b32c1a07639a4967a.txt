Job Description Summary
The UI/UX Designer strengthens the digital commerce ecosystem by producing accessible, brand-consistent, and conversion-driven user experiences across all online platforms including Shopify, Odoo, and Amazon. This role serves as the bridge between creative vision, brand strategy, and technical implementation — ensuring each digital touchpoint reflects the division's identity, elevates brand equity, and drives measurable business growth.
Through end-to-end website design and build, strategic website renewal, unified design systems, conversion-oriented PDPs, and compelling Amazon A+ content, the UI/UX Designer maintains a seamless and scalable digital user journey.
Job Description
Lead end-to-end website design and build for new brand launches (wireframe → prototype → visual design → dev handoff), including Shopify theme customization and Odoo storefront configuration
Translate brand strategy into full site architecture: homepage, category/collection pages, PDP, cart, and checkout flow
Partner with development vendors/agencies to ensure design fidelity from prototype to live site
Lead strategic website renewal initiatives to keep the digital experience current with brand and market standards
Design and maintain a unified design system (component library, typography, color, spacing) applied consistently across Shopify, Odoo, and Amazon
Create high-converting Amazon PDP images and A+ content aligned with brand guidelines
Design responsive, mobile-first layouts optimized for load speed and SEO
Conduct UX audits and usability improvements based on funnel and conversion data
Monitor and report on conversion rate, bounce rate, and page load speed to drive continuous improvement
Work closely with Marketing on campaign landing pages and promotional assets
Collaborate with the GDP/technical team on Shopify–SAP/Odoo integration requirements
Partner with Merchandising and the E-Commerce team on new product launch timelines and PDP requirements
Figma, Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator)
Working knowledge of Shopify Liquid / theme customization, and basic HTML-CSS
Experience with Amazon Seller Central content requirements (A+ Content, Brand Story)
Familiarity with Odoo or similar e-commerce/ERP platforms is a plus
Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end e-commerce site design
Excellent collaboration and communication skills across cross-functional teams
