Role Description The Product Designer will work full time in a remote capacity, collaborating closely with cross-functional partners to design intuitive, visually compelling digital products. Daily work includes conducting user research and UX discovery, defining product flows, developing wireframes, and creating high-fidelity interface designs for web and mobile experiences. The role involves applying design thinking to complex problems, iterating on prototypes based on feedback, and maintaining cohesive design systems and style guides. The Product Designer will participate in design reviews, translate business and user needs into clear design solutions, and ensure consistency across brand and product touchpoints. This role also requires documenting design decisions, preparing assets for handoff to engineering, and contributing to continuous improvement of design processes and standards.
Qualifications
Strong Product Design and Design Thinking skills, with experience solving complex user and business problems.
Proficiency in User Research and UX Research methods, including interviews, usability testing, and synthesizing insights.
Demonstrated Visual Design expertise, including layout, typography, color, and interaction design for digital interfaces.
Experience designing end-to-end experiences for web and mobile products, from wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes.
Comfort with modern design tools (e.g., Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD or similar) and basic understanding of front-end implementation constraints.
Ability to present and communicate design decisions clearly to stakeholders and collaborate effectively in remote teams.
Attention to detail, strong problem-solving abilities, and a portfolio showcasing relevant product and UI/UX work.
Bachelor’s degree in Design, HCI, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience in digital product design.
