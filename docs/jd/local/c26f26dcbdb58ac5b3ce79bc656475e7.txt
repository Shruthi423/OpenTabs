Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and engineers to ensure research insights are integrated into the product development lifecycle.
Advocate for the user throughout the product development process, championing user-centered design principles.
Qualifications
PhD degree in Human-Computer Interaction, Psychology, Sociology, Anthropology, Human Factors or a related field.
5+ years of experience in UX research in industry, preferably within a software or technology company (7+ years of experience if no graduate degree).
Proven expertise in designing and conducting both qualitative and quantitative research methodologies.
A strong portfolio demonstrating your research process, impact, and deliverables.
Proven experience leading hands-on complex research studies involving rigorous methodologies, performing data analysis and synthesis, and report preparation.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills (both written and verbal), including a proven track record of communicating research results for various audiences (e.g., designers, engineers, product managers).
Resourceful: identifies the needs for a project to be successful, and autonomously performs the steps required to acquire tools and methods to achieve success, including reaching out and developing professional relationships with stakeholders and other experts.
Ability to work effectively in a cross-functional team environment
