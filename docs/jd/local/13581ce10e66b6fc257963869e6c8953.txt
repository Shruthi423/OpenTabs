We are working with a pioneering institution at the forefront of risk and safety solutions, dedicated to enhancing how researchers, safety staff, and administrators interact with critical applications. This organization is committed to developing innovative tools that support world-leading research and operational excellence across various sectors.
The Role
Shape the user experience for web applications across campuses and medical centers.
Provide UX and UI design for agile web application development teams.
Own the design of assigned product areas, driving a unified user experience.
Deliver wireframes, interactive prototypes, and detailed, developer-ready design specifications.
Independently plan and conduct usability evaluations and user research activities.
Partner with UX researchers to translate insights into design decisions and validate work with end users.
Bachelor's Degree in a related area or equivalent experience/training.
3+ years of experience designing user interfaces and experiences for responsive web applications (desktop, tablet, mobile).
Proven experience designing user-centric websites and web applications that combine aesthetic quality, usability, and institutional branding, meeting ADA standards.
Proficiency with Figma for interface design, including components, auto-layout, and shared libraries for mockups, prototypes, and design handoffs.
Working familiarity with Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator for supporting imagery and graphics.
Working knowledge of HTML, CSS, and front-end behavior to collaborate effectively with engineers.
