Required Qualifications
3 to 5 years of UX design experience focused on digital products, or equivalent experience
An online portfolio that demonstrates design thinking, problem-solving, and high-quality digital product work
Experience across the user-centered design process, including research synthesis, user flows, wireframes, prototyping, testing, and iteration
Experience designing for web and mobile experiences
Ability to apply design systems, accessibility standards, and UX best practices to create consistent experiences
Strong interaction design and visual communication skills, including layout, hierarchy, typography, and pattern usage
Experience working with cross-functional teams in Agile or similar iterative product development environments
Ability to communicate design rationale clearly and incorporate feedback thoughtfully
Proficiency with Figma or comparable product design tools
Preferred Qualifications
At least 1 year of UX experience designing touch-based mobile interfaces
Experience working with iOS Human Interface Guidelines, Material Design, or similar platform standards
Familiarity with accessibility and inclusive design practices
Experience using design systems and reusable component libraries
Exposure to AI-powered tools or workflows that support design, research, prototyping, or problem-solving
Financial services or other highly regulated industry experience
Comfort collaborating closely with engineers and understanding how design decisions affect implementation
