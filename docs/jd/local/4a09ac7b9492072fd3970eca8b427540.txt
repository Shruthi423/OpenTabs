We are looking for a UI UX Designer to create intuitive digital experiences that support complex, data-intensive applications in Los Angeles, California. In this role, you will blend product design and front-end development to shape interfaces for AI-enabled dashboards, operational tools, and analytical platforms used in high-stakes environments. You will work closely with cross-functional partners to turn intricate workflows into clear, dependable user experiences that are both accessible and production-ready.
• Create user flows, wireframes, clickable prototypes, and refined interface designs that align with established visual standards and product goals.
• Develop and deploy front-end features converting design concepts into reliable, accessible, and maintainable components.
• Define interaction patterns for AI-supported experiences, including approval steps, confidence indicators, traceability cues, and clear responses to uncertain system output.
• Structure navigation, content hierarchy, and task paths for information-heavy applications such as live dashboards, analytical workspaces, and geospatial displays.
• Gather user feedback through interviews, usability studies, experimentation, and usage data analysis, then refine designs based on findings.
• Evolve and document a shared design system across design files and codebases, including reusable components, standards, and usage guidance.
• Collaborate with product, engineering, and technical stakeholders to deliver cohesive solutions across multiple platforms and regulated environments.
• Incorporate accessibility, privacy, and security considerations into interface design and implementation, including support for restricted deployment settings.
• Work on-site in the California office while contributing to iterative product delivery and continuous improvement.
• Use tools such as Figma, JIRA, and GitHub to manage design workflows, coordinate development, and maintain project transparency.
• At least 5 years of experience spanning both UI/UX design and front-end development, supported by a portfolio of launched digital products.
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Human-Computer Interaction, Computer Science, Design, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
• Strong hands-on ability with HTML, CSS, React, TypeScript, and component-based front-end architecture, including attention to testing and performance.
• Advanced proficiency with Figma and experience producing wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity interface designs.
• Demonstrated success building or scaling design systems in both design environments and production code.
• Clear communication skills with the ability to explain design decisions to technical teams, business stakeholders, and non-technical audiences.
• Familiarity with data visualization, mapping, or geospatial interface technologies is highly valued.
• Experience working in defense, intelligence, investment advisory, or other regulated settings is a plus, along with a strong commitment to accessibility and inclusive design.
