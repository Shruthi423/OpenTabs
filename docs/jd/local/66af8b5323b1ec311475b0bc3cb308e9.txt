ABOUT THE ROLE
We’re looking for a Brand Designer to join our creative team. You’ll work directly with our Senior Lead Designer to deliver thoughtful, production-ready creative that helps bring our clients’ brands to life. This isn’t an agency job where you’re creating ads all day or an in-house marketing role where you’re designing the same things over and over.
Every week is different based on the projects UNR has on hand. You might be designing apparel graphics and building a Company Store, creating print materials, developing branded merchandise, helping prepare a Studio Session, or working on a branded environment or website.
If you enjoy variety and like seeing your work become something real, you’ll fit in well here.

One of the things that makes UNR different is The Studio – where many of our client relationships begin.
Clients come in to explore ideas, compare products, review concepts, and build Brand Programs with our team. As a Brand Designer, you’ll help prepare Studio Sessions by creating presentations, mockups, samples, and other creative materials that support the experience. You’ll also have the opportunity to sit in on Studio Sessions, learn how ideas are developed, and grow alongside our Senior Lead Designer.
