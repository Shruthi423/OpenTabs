About the Role:
In the CX/UX Designer role, you will build upon your UX design foundation to take on more responsibility in shaping user experiences. You will engage in designing more detailed wireframes, user flows, and interactive prototypes while contributing to user research and usability testing efforts. This position allows you to further hone your skills and collaborate more closely with cross-functional teams.
