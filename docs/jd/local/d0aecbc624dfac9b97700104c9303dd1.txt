Hands-on experience with Vue.js, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, or design-system implementation, as well as current AI tooling (ex. Cursor, Claude, Codex).
Good interpersonal skills, with experience gathering requirements directly from users and customers, and the ability to create quick prototypes or proof-of-concept UI implementations to validate design ideas.
Solid understanding of data visualization, information architecture, and interaction design for complex technical workflows, as well as the latest design trends and AI tools and how to apply them to design workflows.
