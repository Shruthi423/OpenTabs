About The Role
CodeRabbit's brand is how millions of developers first decide whether to trust us. As our Visual Designer, your home base is CodeRabbit's web presence — coderabbit.ai, product launches, and campaign moments — where you'll design pages, systems, and visuals that get built and shipped, not just handed off. But the impact is bigger than the site: you'll shape how the CodeRabbit brand shows up everywhere it lives, from launch announcements and event experiences to motion, illustration, and social, across digital and physical mediums.
Developers have finely tuned radars for the generic and the overhyped. Our work decides whether CodeRabbit reads as credible craft or as marketing noise — and we care deeply about the details that earn that credibility: the typography, the motion, the pixels, the restraint. We ship fast and learn in the open.
First and foremost, this is a visual design role. Exceptional creativity and design craft for the web are what matter most. You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with engineers who turn design into production code, and you're encouraged to get hands-on in the medium yourself — but we're hiring you for your eye and your ideas.
