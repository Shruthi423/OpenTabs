About The Role
We’re seeking a UX Product Designer to shape the core user experience of Translucent — the AI financial platform transforming how healthcare organizations make business decisions. This is a founding design role. You’ll be the design leader shaping how complex financial and operational workflows become intuitive, conversational, and insight-driven through AI. This role is equal parts product thinking, interaction design, systems design, and visual refinement. You’ll partner with customers, product, and engineering to craft elegant solutions that make healthcare decision-making feel simple.
If you are energized by fast-paced environments, want ownership, and are excited to build new AI-native products from 0→1, this is an opportunity to do career-defining work.
Own product design, UX architecture, prototyping, and visual aesthetic
Partner with product, engineering, and forward-deployed teams to deeply understand customer workflows, pain points, and mental models
Translate complex, data-heavy use cases and workflows into intuitive, clear, and highly-usable product experiences
Define and evolve our design system, visual language, and UX patterns across the platform
Create high-fidelity prototypes to communicate experience direction and enable rapid iteration
Collaborate closely with engineering to ensure design intent is delivered with quality
Drive UX experimentation and validation through user testing, feedback sessions, and live usage signals
Mentor others in product design best practices and help raise the design bar across our team
6–10+ years designing high-quality digital products, ideally in SaaS, analytics platforms, or AI-powered tools for reputable brands
Mastery of UX and interaction design: You can simplify complexity without oversimplifying
Strong visual taste: You sweat the details of typography, hierarchy, and layouts
Experience designing for data-heavy, workflow-driven use cases
Ability to create production-ready Figma designs and prototypes at speed
Comfort working in high-velocity and ambiguity: You can define the problem as you design the solution
Collaborative style: You work fluidly with Product and engineers and know how to drive alignment
Clarity in communication: You can explain the “why” behind your decisions to both technical and non-technical audiences
A portfolio that demonstrates systems thinking, product storytelling, and beautiful execution
Strong ability to rapidly prototype new products or features
Experience designing tools for financial analytics, enterprise workflows, or healthcare users
Experience with conversational interfaces, copilots, agents, or generative AI UX patterns
Prior startup experience where you owned both design strategy and hands-on execution
We're building healthcare's AI financial analyst. We help providers make faster, smarter, and more confident decisions without digging through dashboards or waiting on analyst teams. Backed by top-tier investors, our team combines deep healthcare expertise and technical excellence to reinvent how work gets done in healthcare systems.
Location: New York City (Hybrid - office in Union Square)
