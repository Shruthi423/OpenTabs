About The Role
The role owns the end-to-end user experience design process, transforming complex user workflows and technical constraints into intuitive, accessible, and high-performing product interfaces.
The team works closely with product managers, UX researchers, and front-end engineers to shape features used by millions of people daily, balancing user needs with business goals.
Design user flows, wireframes, low-fidelity prototypes, and polished high-fidelity user interfaces using Figma for web and mobile applications
Conduct user research, usability testing sessions, and interviews to gather qualitative and quantitative insights that drive design decisions
Maintain, scale, and contribute to the unified design system, ensuring consistency in typography, color, components, and interaction patterns
Collaborate directly with front-end engineers during implementation to ensure design integrity and accessibility standards are met
Present design concepts, rationale, and research findings effectively to cross-functional stakeholders and product leadership
3–6 years of professional experience in product design, UX/UI design, or interaction design within a technology company
Expert proficiency in Figma, prototyping tools, and modern design system workflows
Strong portfolio demonstrating deep understanding of user-centered design methodologies, interaction design, and visual polish
Bonus: Experience with front-end code (HTML, CSS, React) and data-driven design optimization
