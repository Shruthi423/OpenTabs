This role offers a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of space technology and software design. You'll gain deep insight into revolutionary manufacturing practices while crafting experiences that accelerate hardware development for companies leading the new space age. You'll help define the future of Boltline both for Stoke Space's internal teams and our growing customer base, ensuring our platform evolves to meet the complex needs of modern aerospace manufacturing.
Partner with product managers, engineers, testers, and Stoke end users to refine requirements and design complex Boltline features
Collaborate with a small team of senior designers to create a unified, consistent user experience and shared product vision
Take end-to-end ownership of features, from problem framing through shipped implementation
Break down ambiguous product problems into concrete, scoped design work that engineering can execute against
Surface trade-offs and roadblocks early, and make defensible calls on scope, sequencing, and quality under schedule pressure
Move quickly from ideas to polished designs in Figma, iterating through fast feedback loops with product team and stakeholders
Validate implemented designs through early release feedback, usability studies, and end user interviews.
Help shape and expand our early-stage design system as the product grows
Qualifications
Portfolio showing strong UI and interaction design skills for complex or technical products, with demonstrated growth in craft or scope over your career
Deep Figma expertise and ability to build clean, scalable systems and prototypes
4+ years of hands-on product design experience, including features you owned end to end
Comfortable running user tests and turning insights into clear, evidence-based design decisions
Proven ability to deliver high-quality work in fast-moving environments with evolving needs
Able to clearly explain design decisions and collaborate effectively with engineering, product, and other partners
Experience working effectively in a highly collaborative small design team
Strong communicator who can work closely with stakeholders and keep everyone aligned
Self-directed and curious — able to dig into technical details and ramp up quickly
Preferred Qualifications
Formal education in Design, Interaction/UX Design or HCI
Experience with complex systems / manufacturing / industrial software
Knowledge of aerospace/manufacturing industries
Familiarity with front-end development technologies
Experience designing early-stage B2B software products
