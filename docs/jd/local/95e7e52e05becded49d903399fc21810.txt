Location: National Capital Region (NCR) or San Antonio, TX
Nightwing is seeking a UX Expert. The User Experience Analyst ensures that all system interfaces maximize user productivity while minimizing training requirements. This position applies user-centered design principles throughout the development lifecycle, conducting research, creating designs, and validating solutions through continuous testing and feedback. The UX Analyst bridges the gap between technical capabilities and user needs, ensuring that the unified solution delivers intuitive, efficient interfaces for operators conducting offensive cyber operations.
Conduct user research through interviews, surveys, and observational studies
Develop user personas, journey maps, and workflow analyses
Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
Design intuitive information architecture aligned with user mental models
Conduct usability testing with representative end users
Develop and maintain design systems with reusable components
Produce quarterly UI/UX reports with actionable recommendations
Collaborate with development teams to ensure proper design implementation
Gather and analyze user feedback from all deployment environments
Support SAFe ceremonies with user experience insights
Required Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Human-Computer Interaction, Design, Psychology, or related field (or equivalent)
Demonstrated experience conducting user research in classified environments
Proficiency with design tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, or similar)
Experience creating wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
Strong understanding of accessibility standards and responsive design
Background in offensive cyber operations or related mission areas
Portfolio demonstrating user-centered design process and outcomes
The salary associated with this position ($122,000-$253,000) is commensurate with the selected candidate’s qualifications, years of relevant experience, and demonstrated level of expertise. Compensation will be determined based on these factors to ensure alignment with skills, responsibilities, and market standards.
Nightwing offers medical, vision and dental insurance coverage in addition to a 401k plan, PTO, Holidays, and additional insurances.
