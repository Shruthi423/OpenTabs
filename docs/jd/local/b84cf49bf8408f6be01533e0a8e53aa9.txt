Product Designer at Surge: Crafting User-Centric Design Solutions
Surge is seeking a creative and skilled Product Designer to join our innovative team. In this role, you'll utilize your expertise in design tools such as Sketch, Adobe Creative Suite, and Figma to develop user-centric products that align with business goals. At Surge, your contribution will be pivotal in shaping the design and user experience of our products. Join us to be part of a team that values creativity, innovation, and the power of design in transforming ideas into tangible solutions.
At Surge, we're dedicated to innovation in design and technology. Our culture fosters creativity, collaboration, and a commitment to excellence. Joining Surge means being part of a team where your skills in product design, user experience, and user interface are highly valued. We are passionate about creating products that not only meet user needs but also exceed their expectations, making a significant impact in the market.
Role of Product Designer at Surge: Collaborative Design, User Research
Lead the design of innovative products, ensuring they are user-friendly and align with business objectives.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate design concepts and user feedback into the product development process.
Conduct user research, create prototypes, and perform usability testing to refine design solutions.
Utilize a variety of design tools to create wireframes, visual designs, and interactive prototypes.
Apply design principles and best practices to ensure a cohesive user experience across all product touchpoints.
Essential Skills for Surge’s Product Designer: Design Principles, Prototyping
Proficiency in design tools like Sketch, Adobe Creative Suite, and Figma.
Strong understanding of design principles, user experience, and product development processes.
Experience in user research, prototyping, and usability testing.
Ability to work collaboratively in a team and communicate design ideas effectively.
A portfolio showcasing previous work and design solutions.
Advanced Product Design Skills at Surge: HTML/CSS Knowledge, Motion Design
Knowledge of HTML/CSS for understanding web and mobile design constraints.
Experience or interest in motion design to enhance user interaction.
Ability to adapt to emerging design trends and tools.
Surge offers a competitive salary range of $70,000 to $100,000, based on experience and expertise in product design. We value our team members and are committed to providing a supportive and dynamic work environment that fosters professional growth and development.
