Strata is driven by our people solving problems together. We embrace learning, collaboration, and continuous career growth. Together, we lift our customers, our products, our company, and our community. As a UX Researcher, you will work directly with our user experience team to help many of the nation's leading hospitals, universities, and financial institutions make better decisions with their financial, operational, and strategic data so they can care for their communities.
This UX Researcher role is all about working with Product Managers and UX Designers to solve complex problems by uncovering the needs and pain points of our users and turning insights into actionable solutions and product decisions. Some examples of how you'll help:
Conduct generative and evaluative research to uncover user needs, behaviors, and motivations, guiding the product team through all stages of product development
Be the voice of the user in cross-functional team discussions, advocating for user needs while balancing business objectives and constraints
Help foster a user-centric culture by refining unclear questions into clear research opportunities, sharing insights across teams, and ensuring those insights help shape product decisions from discovery through launch
Feel comfortable sharing your expertise, offering research advice in group settings, growing into a trusted research partner to your stakeholders and team members.
Fearlessly dig into existing research and new research you generate to understand our users' needs
Contribute to problem discovery activities, with your design and product manager peers, while learning how to refine ambiguous questions into clearer goals.
Do all the research things: Create research plans, coordinate recruitment, talk to our users, share relevant insights with stakeholders, connect insights across studies and teams, with support from senior researchers.
Become an expert in our research repository and help maintain it so our product teams have a source of truth to influence their decisions.
Ask tough questions, identify assumptions, discuss new ideas, and remain open to opposing viewpoints
Actively contribute to the growth and development of the research function
Show initiative in end-to-end ownership of research studies, while seeking feedback and guidance from senior team members as you stretch into strategic work.
Brainstorm research questions with other research team members
Work with your product manager and design partners to uncover where there are gaps in knowledge that you can contribute to
Review findings and recommendations with your design partner
Geek out over the latest trends in research and actively share your learnings with the team to spark discussion
Guide your team through the pros and cons of your research plan and questions along with your decision-making process
