A client with Kforce is seeking a Product Designer II to join their team in Mountain View, CA. This is an onsite hybrid position in office 3 days a week. Overview: The client Identity design team is looking for a Senior Product Design Contract Worker to help shape onboarding and identity verification experiences across the client. In this role, you will design experiences that help customers join business accounts, verify their identity when needed, and move seamlessly across the client's products. You will focus on cross-product onboarding, business account invitations, and adaptive identity verification that reduces unnecessary friction while meeting security and regulatory requirements. Key Tasks:
Design intuitive end-to-end experiences that balance customer needs, business goals, and technical constraints across web and mobile
Design onboarding, business account invitation, and identity verification experiences using the client's Design System components and patterns
Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs in Figma, using AI-assisted tools where they improve exploration, iteration, or documentation
Communicate customer journeys, design decisions, and recommendations through presentations and design artifacts
Partner with Product Management, Engineering, Data Science, and User Research throughout the product development process
Deliver detailed specifications, support implementation, and review experiences before launch
Test and refine concepts using customer feedback, usability testing, and product data, working independently when dedicated research support is not available
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Human-Computer Interaction, Product Design, Graphic Design, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
Strong senior-level product designer with 5+ years of experience leading complex product initiatives from concept through implementation
Portfolio demonstrating end-to-end ownership of complex workflows in identity, onboarding, platform, enterprise, or fintech products
Strong systems thinking and the ability to simplify complex customer journeys into intuitive, scalable experiences
Experience designing across multiple products or shared platform capabilities
Ability to work through ambiguous problems with evolving technical, business, security, or regulatory requirements
Strong collaboration, facilitation, and communication skills, with experience driving alignment across cross-functional teams
Proficiency in Figma and familiarity with AI-assisted design tools such as Claude, Cursor, or similar tools
Experience with identity, authentication, authorization, permissions, or enterprise account systems is strongly preferred
