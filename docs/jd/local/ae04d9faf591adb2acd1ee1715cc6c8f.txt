We're looking for a Brand Designer to own the execution of our brand and carry it into the product. You'll work closely with the Founding Designer, who sets brand direction, and you'll be responsible for producing the work: launch and campaign creative, the marketing site, social decks, conference materials, and internal templates.
A key part of this role is bringing brand back into the product. Hypha's application is data-dense, built around tables, spreads, citations, and dashboards. You'll translate brand decisions into product tokens, components, iconography, and illustration so that the product and the brand read as the same company.
This role is ideal for someone who is strong in execution, systems thinking, and rapid iteration.
