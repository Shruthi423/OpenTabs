Marriott International is seeking a FLEX Product Designer, to create intuitive, engaging, and customer-centered experiences within the Marriott Bonvoy App. This role partners closely with Product Management, Engineering, Research, Analytics, Content Design, and Design teams to design seamless mobile experiences across the travel journey, including trip planning, booking management, stay preparation, in-stay interactions, and post-stay engagement. The position is responsible for transforming customer insights, business requirements, and technical considerations into intuitive design solutions that balance customer needs with business objectives. This role requires strong UX, interaction design, and visual design skills, along with a passion for creating simple, elegant experiences that improve both customer satisfaction and business outcomes.
Bachelor's degree in User Experience Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Graphic Design, a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
5+ years of experience in User Experience Design, Product Design, Interaction Design, or a related discipline designing consumer-facing digital products.
Experience creating mobile application experiences using user-centered design methodologies, wireframing, prototyping, interaction design, and visual design tools such as Figma.
Experience designing travel, hospitality, loyalty, commerce, marketplace, or subscription-based digital experiences.
Experience working within enterprise design systems and creating scalable design solutions.
Experience leveraging customer research, usability testing, analytics, and accessibility principles to inform design decisions.
