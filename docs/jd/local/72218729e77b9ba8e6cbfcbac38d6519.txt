## About the Role
We are looking for a creative and user-focused UX Designer to design intuitive, accessible, and engaging digital experiences. You will work closely with product, development, and business teams to understand user needs and translate them into effective design solutions.
The ideal candidate combines strong visual thinking with user research, problem-solving, and an understanding of modern digital products.
* Conduct user research and analyze user behavior.
* Create user personas, journey maps, and user flows.
* Design wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity interfaces.
* Develop intuitive and user-friendly website and application experiences.
* Collaborate with developers and product teams throughout the design process.
* Conduct usability testing and incorporate user feedback.
* Identify usability issues and recommend improvements.
* Maintain consistency across digital products and platforms.
* Contribute to design systems and reusable UI components.
* Create responsive designs for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* Monitor UX trends and emerging design practices.
* Support continuous improvement of existing products.
## Required Qualifications
* Bachelor's degree in UX Design, UI/UX, Graphic Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field.
* Experience designing websites, applications, or digital products.
* Strong understanding of UX principles and user-centered design.
* Experience with tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or similar platforms.
* Knowledge of wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing.
* Understanding of responsive and accessible design.
* Strong communication and collaboration skills.
* Excellent problem-solving and analytical abilities.
* Strong attention to detail.
* Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines.
* UX Research
* User Journey Mapping
* Information Architecture
* Wireframing
* Prototyping
* Usability Testing
* UI Design
* Figma
* Design Systems
* Responsive Design
* Accessibility
* Interaction Design
* User-Centered Design
* Mobile UX
* Web Design
The ideal candidate is a creative problem solver who understands both users and business requirements. You should be comfortable turning complex problems into simple, intuitive experiences and working collaboratively with cross-functional teams.
A strong portfolio demonstrating UX research, design processes, wireframes, prototypes, and final product experiences is highly desirable.
We are committed to creating an inclusive and respectful workplace. All qualified candidates are encouraged to apply and will be considered based on their skills, experience, qualifications, and ability to contribute to the role.
