HIFI is a stablecoin infrastructure company building technology for the next-generation of financial products. We build developer tools that allow value to be programmed—with rules, behavior, and logic embedded directly at the asset level. Institutions and enterprises already use our platform and API's to move billions of dollars in production environments around the world. But we view this as the beginning rather than the end.
Founded in 2022, our organization is flat and our team is talent dense. We are in person at our office in Flatiron and are intentional about who we work with. We've raised $33 million from world-class investors and recently closed our Series A lead by Left Lane Capital.
HIFI is building its Design function for the first time. As the company scales from an early-stage startup into a long-term, category-defining platform, this role will define how HIFI looks, sounds, and shows up everywhere the world sees us - social, web, product marketing, and beyond.
The Brand Designer works directly with the Marketing and Product teams to own HIFI's outward-facing identity. This is a hands-on execution role. You'll be the one designing and shipping the assets the brand runs on.
Define our motion language: timing, easing, pacing, and the behaviors that make our animation recognizably ours across product and marketing surfaces. Translate complex payment orchestration infrastructure into simple, compelling narratives that resonate with developers and enterprise teams.
Build a data visualization system for transaction flows, settlement timing, and coverage maps that scales across marketing and product.
Lead creative for product launches end to end, from the announcement video and hero animation down to the social cuts, docs diagrams, and sales enablement docs.
Build and maintain the product demo. This is a recurring deliverable used in sales calls, on the website, at conferences, and in outbound. It should be the clearest explanation of the product that exists anywhere.
Bring the website to life with animation that is performant and serves the story.
Design the assets around our conference calendar: booth graphics, event signage, partner co-marketing collateral, and printed material.
Create reusable templates, components, and export presets so the rest of the team can seamlessly produce on-brand assets.
Partner with product designers and engineering to carry brand into the product itself: empty states, onboarding moments, micro-interactions, and dashboard states.
Evolve the visual identity as we grow, including type, color, illustration, iconography, and the rules that govern where each applies.
3-6 years of experience in brand, digital, or product marketing design, ideally at high-growth startups, fintech, or crypto
Track record of owning brand/design output end-to-end in fast-moving, resource-constrained environments
Fluent across a broad toolkit: Figma (web/UI), After Effects or similar (motion), and modern design systems
Comfortable with ambiguity and building process from scratch
Comfortable translating technical, abstract concepts (onchain transactions, payment rails, APIs) into intuitive visual and written stories
Strong eye for typography, color, and brand system, not just individual assets
Front-end fluency (HTML/CSS or webflow-type tools) to ship web design without heavy engineering handoff
