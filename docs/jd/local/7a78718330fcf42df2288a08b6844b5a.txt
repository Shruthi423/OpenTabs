Aquent Studios is the creative agency that provides scalability with world-class services to Qualcomm’s in-house studio. This collaboration will help shape how one of the world’s most innovative technology brands shows up in culture and in the market. You’ll help build breakthrough campaigns and bring cutting-edge technology stories to life for audiences around the world.
About The Role
Aquent Studios is seeking a Visual Designer, Brand to join a small embedded creative team supporting Qualcomm’s brand portfolio, including the foundational corporate brand and product brands Snapdragon, Dragonwing, and Dragonfly. Reporting to the Creative Director, Brand, you’ll bring design systems, typography, and visual identity to life across a wide range of brand touchpoints, from key visuals and photography-driven layouts to templates that scale across the organization. This is a hands-on execution role for a designer who cares deeply about craft and consistency.
What You’ll Do
Design within and help maintain Qualcomm’s design systems, applying typography, layout, and visual identity guidelines consistently across projects
Produce key visuals, campaign assets, templates, and brand collateral across the Qualcomm master brand and its Snapdragon, Dragonwing, and Dragonfly sub-brands
Work with photography, sourcing, art directing, cropping, and retouching images to fit brand guidelines and key visual language
Partner with the Creative Director, Brand to translate creative direction into polished, on-brand execution
Build and update templates, guideline documents, and asset libraries that help the broader organization apply the brand correctly
Collaborate with other designers on the team, sharing feedback and maintaining a consistent quality bar
Take feedback from creative and stakeholder reviews and iterate quickly and thoughtfully
Stay current on typography, layout, and visual design trends relevant to the brand’s category
3–6 years of experience in visual or brand design, ideally within an in-house, agency, or embedded team setting
Strong typography and layout skills, with an eye for detail and consistency
Experience working within established design systems and brand guidelines
Comfort working with photography — direction, selection, cropping, and basic retouching
A portfolio that shows range across brand touchpoints (not just one type of deliverable)
Proficiency in standard design tools (e.g., Figma, Adobe Creative Suite)
Strong collaboration skills and comfort receiving and applying creative direction
Experience designing for technology, semiconductor, or consumer electronics brands
Experience working across a multi-brand or sub-brand portfolio
Familiarity with maintaining brand asset libraries or design system documentation (e.g., Figma libraries, brand portals)
