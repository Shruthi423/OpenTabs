The Role
As a Visual Designer, you will shape how Kyndryl shows up across key brand touchpoints by creating clear, compelling, and consistent visual experiences. You will translate business, customer, and brand requirements into high-quality design solutions that strengthen brand recognition, enhance communication, and support business goals. Working closely with marketing, communications, experience, and product teams, you will balance hands-on design execution with collaboration to deliver impactful visual assets across multiple channels.
What You Will Do
Create visual assets for digital, social, web, print, presentation, and campaign needs.
Translate briefs, content, and business objectives into engaging visual designs and layouts.
Apply brand standards, design systems, and templates to ensure consistency across customer and internal touchpoints.
Design graphics, mockups, high-fidelity screens, presentations, and other visual assets that support storytelling and business goals.
Collaborate with writers, marketers, UX designers, developers, and stakeholders to deliver effective visual solutions.
Adapt existing design systems and assets for new formats, platforms, and audiences.
Use feedback, testing, and data insights to refine and improve designs.
Prepare production-ready assets that meet quality, accessibility, and delivery requirements.
