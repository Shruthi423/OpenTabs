Role Description The UI/UX & Product Designer is a full-time role responsible for designing intuitive, engaging digital products from concept through execution. This hybrid position is based in Roseville, CA, with a full on-site collaboration. Day-to-day responsibilities include conducting user and UX research, translating insights into wireframes and high-fidelity visual designs, and iterating on design concepts based on stakeholder and user feedback. The designer will collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and other team members to define product requirements, create interaction flows, and ensure design solutions are feasible and aligned with business goals. Additional tasks include participating in design workshops, maintaining design systems, presenting work to internal and external stakeholders, and contributing to continuous improvement of design processes and standards.
Qualifications
Strong Visual Design skills, including layout, typography, color, and component-based UI design.
Proficiency in Design Thinking, with the ability to frame problems, ideate solutions, and prototype user-centered experiences.
Experience in User Research and UX Research, including planning studies, conducting interviews or usability tests, and synthesizing findings.
Hands-on Product Design experience, from discovery and requirements gathering through to interaction design and collaboration on implementation.
Advanced skills with modern design and prototyping tools (e.g., Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, or similar).
Ability to communicate design decisions clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders and to incorporate feedback constructively.
Experience working in cross-functional teams with engineering, product management, and QA in an agile or iterative development environment.
Portfolio demonstrating end-to-end product design work, including research, concept development, interaction design, and visual execution.
Familiarity with accessibility standards and designing inclusive, responsive interfaces for web and mobile platforms.
