Who You Are
A builder. You go from problem to prototype to shipped product. You don't wait for permission, a sprint planning meeting, or a ticket. You see the gap and you close it.
Taste-obsessed. You've studied the best — Ramp, Airbnb, the fastest-scaling companies — and you have real opinions about what makes them great. Your eye is trained. What you ship is beautiful and considered, even when you ship it fast.
Intuition-driven. You don't hide behind process. You've done enough reps to trust your gut, move quickly, and be right far more often than you're wrong. When the data isn't there, you still move.
AI-native. You're already using AI every day — not as a toy, but as core infrastructure. You see it as the thing that turns one designer into ten.
You care about business outcomes that align with customer satisfaction. Every pixel, every interaction, every flow is crafted around how your customers will actually feel when they use the product.
