The Product Designer will execute the creation of AI-enabled, user-centric design experiences across Pharmacy Management, Clinical Service and Inventory Optimization technology products. This role combines human-centered design practices, strategic product thinking, and AI innovation to accelerate the delivery of scalable product solutions that drive value for customers and achieve business outcomes.
This role will drive end-to-end product design initiatives, research insights, and design optimization from discovery to launch for products with minimal supervision. Partnering closely with Product, Design, and Engineering Leadership, this individual will leverage extensive AI prototyping capabilities, expertise in Figma and design systems, and exceptional UI/UX design skills to deliver seamless design artifacts that enrich the customer experience.
Reporting to the VP of Product, this role will be responsible for driving consistency, scalability, and usability across a complex portfolio of products. Within the product organization, the Product Designer will shape product design and product strategy and advocate for intuitive integration of AI into the design workflow to creatively contribute to product outcomes. The ideal candidate possesses expert visual, interaction, and systems design capabilities and effectively communicates design recommendations that drive leadership decisions.
McKesson Pharmacy System (MPS) is an independent business within the larger McKesson Commercial Strategy & Innovation (CSI) organization.
Execute the end-to-end design for AI- enabled product experiences for platforms including web, mobile, and other channels from discovery to launch, accelerating product, design, delivery, and optimization.
Translate user needs into intuitive workflows, interaction models, and scalable solutions aligned with the product strategy.
Decompose customer needs and translate them into intuitive, scalable product designs that tie back to the intent of the product strategy.
Conduct qualitative and quantitative user research to identify customer needs, synthesizing the insights and behavioral data into actionable recommendations.
Validate concepts through usability testing and define success metrics that inform product design decisions.
Define cross-portfolio design systems to drive consistency across components, accessibility standards, and interaction patterns.
Create AI-driven, reusable frameworks and patterns that highlight product recommendations, product development, and improve user experience quality.
