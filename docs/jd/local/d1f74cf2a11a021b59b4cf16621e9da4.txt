JOB DESCRIPTION
The Tax Digital Transformation & Innovation practice at BDO USA drives the success of our business through intuitive and human-first digital experiences that our employees and clients engage with. We're elevating the way tax professionals deliver services to our clients and the way we help them manage their taxes and tax strategies.
The Tax Digital Transformation & Innovation practice at BDO USA delivers human-centered digital experiences for tax professionals and clients. The Senior Product Design Engineer bridges product design and front-end engineering, transforming complex, data-heavy tax workflows into intuitive, scalable, and accessible experiences.
This role is approximately 70% engineering and 30% design. The ideal candidate can move seamlessly from concept and prototype to production-quality implementation, partnering closely with Product, Engineering, and Design teams across global time zones. You will contribute to our design system, build high-performance user interfaces, and help accelerate product delivery through modern AI-assisted workflows.
Translates product concepts and Figma designs into production-ready user experiences
Builds responsive, accessible, and performant front-end applications
Contributes to the evolution of shared design systems and reusable component libraries
Collaborates asynchronously with globally distributed teams in a follow-the-sun model
Prototypes concepts rapidly to validate ideas and improve decision-making
Leverages AI-assisted tools and workflows to accelerate design, development, testing, and documentation
Uses qualitative and quantitative insights to improve user experiences
Champions accessibility, usability, and consistency across products
Mentors peers and contribute to design and engineering best practices
Acts as a career advisor and mentor as assigned
Qualifications, Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities:
Bachelor’s degree and five (5) or more years of experience in product design engineering, front-end development or UX engineering required; OR High School Diploma/GED and six (6) or more years of experience in product design engineering, front-end development or UX engineering required
Bachelor’s Degree in focus in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Computer Science, preferred
Experience translating design concepts into production-quality interfaces, preferred
Experience collaborating with cross-functional product teams, preferred
Strong portfolio demonstrating product thinking, interaction design, and front-end implementation, preferred
Fluency in modern IDEs (e.g. Visual Studio, Cursor, etc.), required
React, Angular, TypeScript, Next.js, TailwindCSS, and Figma, preferred
Design systems and component-based architecture, preferred
Accessibility standards and responsive design, preferred
REST APIs and modern web application architecture, preferred
Familiarity with AI tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, and v0, preferred
Knowledge of leading practices in frontend execution with a data-driven approach to informing decisions
Strong communication and collaboration skills
Comfortable operating in ambiguous and fast-moving environments
Strong problem-solving and organizational abilities
