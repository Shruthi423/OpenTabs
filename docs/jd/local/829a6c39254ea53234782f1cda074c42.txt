We're redefining the future of legal work with AI-powered Augmented Intelligence, enabling Fortune 500 legal teams to move faster, manage risk more effectively, and unlock new business value. Backed by $105M in Series A funding led by General Catalyst, we're building a category-defining platform that blends AI-driven automation with human expertise, transforming legal from a cost center into a strategic growth driver.
The Role
We're looking for a Product Designer with 5-10 years of experience who brings a strong visual design sensibility combined with solid product thinking. You should be comfortable working in a fast-paced, ambiguous startup environment designing AI-powered enterprise products, with a track record of initiating ideas and shipping high-quality designs end-to-end. Bonus points if you've worked at consumer-quality companies (like Pinterest, Airbnb, or newer AI startups) or have experience with AI product design.
What You'll Do
Own the full design lifecycle for new product verticals — from ideation and concept exploration through to polished, production-ready mocks and prototypes in Figma
Collaborate closely with PMs and engineers to understand technical constraints, brainstorm solutions, and ensure what ships matches the design vision
Create high-fidelity visual designs including UI, icons, and marketing materials that meet a consumer-grade quality bar for enterprise users
Work hands-on with engineers during implementation to maintain design quality and iterate based on feedback
Conduct lightweight UX research when needed — customer interviews, usability tests — and synthesize findings into actionable design decisions
Proactively generate and champion new ideas rather than waiting to be told what to design, bringing creative leadership to the product team
Work policy: On-site at our Palo Alto, CA office five days per week, with some flexibility for personal appointments coordinated with your manager
Equity: Equity compensation included as part of the total package
