The UI/UX Designer is responsible for end-to-end product design across BI Incorporated's product portfolio. Collaborates with the product team to conduct user research, create interaction and visual design solutions, build prototypes, run usability testing, and synthesize user feedback into design improvements that address our clients’ needs and help us grow our customer base.
The UI/UX Designer gathers and evaluates user requirements in collaboration with product managers and engineers.
Evaluates the usability of existing applications to identify opportunities for improvement.
Conducts user testing of applications, software and websites.
The UI/UX Designer creates and implements interaction models, user flows, and user interface specifications.
Partners with product managers and engineers throughout delivery, clearly communicating design intent, specifications, and rationale to support accurate implementation.
The UI/UX Designer illustrates design ideas using tools like storyboards, process flows and sitemaps.
Designs graphic user interface elements, like menus, tabs and widgets.
The UI/UX Designer builds page navigation buttons and search fields.
Develops UI mockups and prototypes that clearly illustrate how sites function and look like.
Prepares and presents rough drafts to internal teams and key stakeholders.
Identifies and troubleshoots UX problems (e.g. responsiveness).
Conducts user research and synthesizes findings into actionable design direction.
Contributes to and maintains design standards and documentation.
