Support prototyping, testing, troubleshooting, and validation of designs to ensure performance, safety, functionality, and manufacturability.
Evaluate materials, components, fabrication methods, tooling, fixtures, and assembly processes to improve production flow and reduce rework.
Review customer requirements, technical specifications, and application needs to develop practical engineering solutions for thermal processing equipment.
Assist with continuous improvement projects related to manufacturing processes, quality, standard work, product documentation, and engineering efficiency.
Partner with cross-functional teams to support product launches, custom orders, design revisions, and production readiness.
Ensure designs align with applicable safety standards, quality expectations, customer requirements, and internal manufacturing capabilities.
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Product Design, Manufacturing Engineering, or a related technical field preferred.
3+ years of experience in product design, mechanical design, manufacturing engineering, industrial equipment design, or custom engineered products.
Strong experience with 3D CAD software such as SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Creo, Inventor, or similar design tools.
Ability to create and interpret engineering drawings, specifications, bills of materials, tolerances, and manufacturing documentation.
Working knowledge of mechanical design principles, fabrication, sheet metal, welding, machining, assembly, electrical/mechanical components, and design for manufacturability.
Experience in industrial equipment, thermal processing equipment, furnaces, ovens, heaters, machinery, or custom manufacturing is a plus.
Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to balance product performance, manufacturability, cost, safety, and customer requirements.
Effective communication and collaboration skills with the ability to work across engineering, production, sales, purchasing, and leadership teams.
Why Join Thermcraft? Thermcraft offers the opportunity to design real products that are built on-site and used by customers around the world. As a 100% employee-owned ESOP company in Winston-Salem, NC, we are looking for people who want to contribute ideas, solve problems, support manufacturing, and take pride in building high-quality thermal processing solutions. If you are a hands-on engineer who enjoys product design, CAD, manufacturing, and continuous improvement, we encourage you to apply.
