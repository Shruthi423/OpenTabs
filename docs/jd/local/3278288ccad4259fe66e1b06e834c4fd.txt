This role is separate from the wider Design team’s day-to-day client work (brand, campaign, video, social, and print), which continues as it does today. Until now, design work for LOCOMOTIVE’s internal tools has been picked up ad hoc alongside client projects. That includes the design system, component library, and the user-facing documentation that keeps everything on brand. This role takes primary ownership of all of it.
The role
You’ll primarily work on client AI-powered website designs, UI/UX, interactive content and interfaces for LOCOMOTIVE’s internally built software, website and tools, as well as the client-facing versions of that software where we’ve identified commercial opportunities. Alongside that, you’ll shape AI empowered web design work, moving between Figma and HTML to take concepts from first sketch to a build ready state in WordPress, Builder.io, and similar platforms.
Your job is to be the connective tissue between design and UI/UX best practice and the needs of the product or website. You’ll sit within the Design team and report to the Head of Creative Services, giving you a home base in design craft and standards while your day to day focus runs through Technology/R&D and Web Development. Technology in this space moves quickly. AI tooling, design systems, and web capabilities are all changing at a pace that rewards curiosity over certainty. We’re looking for someone who treats that as the interesting part of the job, not a disruption to it: someone who tests new tools before being asked to, questions their own assumptions, and adapts their process as the landscape shifts under them.
What you’ll do
Design UI/UX for LOCOMOTIVE’s internally built software solutions and other tools as the product set grows
Extend that design work into client facing versions of internal tools where a commercial opportunity has been identified, adapting for external users and use cases
Design AI-powered web experiences end to end, using Figma and prompt-based design tools to turn concepts directly into build-ready HTML/CSS for implementation in WordPress, Builder.io, and similar platforms
Collaborate with Technology/R&D on product direction, feasibility, and the technical constraints that shape good design decisions
Work directly with Web Development on implementation, ensuring designs translate cleanly into build and holding quality through that handoff
Maintain design consistency across LOCOMOTIVE’s internal tools, working toward a shared design language and reusable component library, style guide and clear usage documentation so software developers can build on brand without needing a full design pass each time
Apply core UX practice to every project: information architecture, interaction design, core usability principles, and accessibility from the first draft rather than as a late pass
Contribute to or run usability testing where relevant, and use the findings to iterate rather than treat first drafts as final
Document design decisions and specs clearly enough for software developers to build from without repeated back and forth, including states, edge cases, and responsive behavior
Stay ahead of AI and web design tooling, testing new platforms and workflows and bringing back a business case for what’s worth adopting
Represent design thinking in product and technical conversations where no dedicated designer would otherwise be in the room
Create working HTML/CSS prototypes using prompt-based design tools, taking ideas beyond static Figma layouts before development begins
Define reusable design tokens, components, states, and interaction patterns that can be shared across Figma, coded interfaces, and AI development workflows
Maintain alignment between the Figma library and implemented component system as both evolve
Develop prompting patterns, reference examples, and design-system guidance that help AI tools generate consistent, on-brand interfaces
Review AI-generated interfaces critically and refine them to meet LOCOMOTIVE
