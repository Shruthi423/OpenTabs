About The Role
As designer at Orb, you’ll drive the end-to-end design process, from early discovery research through to implementation. You’ll own solutioning and vision setting for Orb’s revenue reporting and invoicing products in partnership with your team
Our team is thoughtful about what makes a world-class user experience. We value opinionated defaults with strong safeguards–and will look to you to help guide our users to the best outcomes.
Lead user research sessions—including discovery interviews, contextual inquiries, and usability tests—to deeply understand user workflows, uncover pain points, and validate solutions through prototype feedback
Rapidly iterate on solutions through low- and high-fidelity prototypes, using each round to de-risk assumptions and sharpen the problem definition
Partner with engineers and product managers from day zero to shape the direction of new initiatives, balancing user needs, technical constraints, and business goals
Frame ambiguous problems into clear design opportunities, defining success criteria alongside product and engineering partners
Actively participate in design critique—soliciting feedback early, offering thoughtful critique to peers, and raising the quality bar across the team
Partner closely with engineers throughout implementation to ensure the shipped experience matches design intent, and make pragmatic trade-offs when it doesn't
Contribute to and evolve the design system alongside other designers—proposing new patterns, refining existing components, and championing consistency across surfaces
Communicate design decisions clearly to cross-functional stakeholders, grounding recommendations in user research, data, and craft principles
Own the end-to-end quality of your work, from initial research through post-launch measurement and iteration
About You
5+ years of professional design experience, preferably in B2B SaaS

You are a strong communicator that thrives with cross-functional collaboration and are excited about collaborating with product and engineering counterparts
You work iteratively, sharing early and often
You think in systems and are constantly evaluating how your design decisions interact with the entire product ecosystem
You are able to break large design initiatives into smaller, shippable pieces without sacrificing usability
Get excited about content design/UX copywriting
Have experience working on products that are designed for accounting and finance personas
