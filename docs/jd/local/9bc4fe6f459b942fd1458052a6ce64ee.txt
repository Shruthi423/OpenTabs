About The Role
We are looking for a UX Designer to drive meaningful improvements to our product’s usability and user experience. In this role, you will tackle real, identified friction points in the application—redesigning flows, rethinking interactions, and systematically reducing the cumbersome aspects of the tool that slow users down.
Ease of use is one of our most important competitive differentiators against entrenched players whose tools are notoriously difficult to navigate. Your work will directly strengthen that advantage. You’ll operate close to the product and engineering teams, often pairing with engineers to move quickly from design to implementation—making your impact tangible and fast.
What You'll Do
Audit and evaluate existing UX problems across the application, identifying the highest-impact areas of friction for users.
Redesign and re-implement UX elements and user flows to reduce complexity and improve overall usability.
Translate team-identified UX issues into concrete design solutions, from low-fidelity concepts through final specs.
Partner closely with engineers to ensure designs are implemented accurately and efficiently, including hands-on pairing during build-out.
Collaborate with the product team to prioritize UX improvements alongside broader product goals.
Establish and iterate on design patterns that promote consistency and ease of use throughout the product.
Solicit and incorporate feedback from stakeholders and users to continuously refine the experience.
