Explore new UI patterns for working with agents, generated content, flexible canvases, and product experiences that learn from user preferences over time. You’ll help us figure out what feels intuitive, powerful, and not like every other chat-based AI product.
Who you are
We're looking for a designer who embodies the following
Early career, high slope. You might be a new grad or a few years into your career. What matters most is that you learn unusually fast, care deeply about the work, and show signs of becoming excellent.
Comfortable making reasonable decisions. You don’t need perfect information before moving forward. You ask good questions, use judgment, and can make progress even when the path is not fully defined.
Strong product instincts. You care about how the thing works, not just how it looks. You notice when a flow feels confusing, when an AI output feels generic, when a screen is trying to do too much, or when a detail actually matters.
Fast, open, and coachable. You show work early, take feedback seriously, and come back with something better. You want to be pushed, and you want to get very good.
Energized by the pace. We work in person in New York City, five days a week, and the product is moving quickly. The right person will find that intense, clarifying, and fun.
If these are the things that make you excited
You want a mature design org with established rituals and clean handoffs.
You prefer to receive scoped requirements rather than shape the problem.
You believe business software just needs to be usable.
You are interested in AI as a feature, but not as a new material for designing software.
You want remote-first work. We work together in person in New York City because the product is being invented through fast loops between design, engineering, founders, customers, and the product itself.

We’re backed by Sequoia, YC, Insight, and world-class GTM operators. More importantly, we’re building for a moment when the shape of work is changing fast and the design patterns have not been settled yet.
