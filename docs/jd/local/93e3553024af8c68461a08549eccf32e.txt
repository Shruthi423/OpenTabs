Are you ready to take the reins on significant user experience initiatives, transforming challenging problems into elegant solutions? In this pivotal design role, you won’t just design; you’ll own outcomes, define scalable strategies, and evangelize a long-term vision that shapes the future of product development. This is your chance to make a profound impact, guiding design momentum across multiple efforts and influencing product strategy with your visionary perspective, contributing directly to products used by millions globally.
What You’ll Do
Own the end-to-end user experience outcomes for mature projects, complex programs, or emerging areas, navigating challenges with limited precedent to define scalable and sustainable approaches.
Develop and evangelize long-term user experience and product strategy for your team, influencing multiple adjacent design efforts and offering alternative perspectives to challenge prevailing thinking.
Serve as a subject-matter expert in user experience design principles, user-centered product development methodologies, design systems, and research tools, applying your expertise to complex problems.
Collaborate closely with engineers to communicate, negotiate, and influence design intention, guiding the implementation of detailed design specifications, mock-ups, and product models.
Drive improvements in design by leveraging key data and metrics, user experience testing, and stakeholder input, actively engaging partners to achieve product goals.
Build consensus and gain support for design strategy across diverse teams and stakeholders, effectively communicating your approach and anticipating potential challenges.
Contribute to defining project priorities aligned with broader organizational goals, and strategically coordinate resource allocation within your immediate product team.
Create innovative design solutions based on deep user insights, business needs, and industry trends, ensuring they explicitly consider and mitigate unintentional harm while supporting key performance indicators (KPIs).
Drive the implementation of a holistic design process, design system, or design language across various teams or products, developing and generating support for a cohesive design vision.
Guide other user experience designers in creating design concepts for product ideation and user research, or conduct independent research to inform and validate design directions.
Apply, adapt, and define robust user experience design frameworks, including principles, patterns, and systems, leveraging writing and design tools to visualize ideas, develop mock-ups, and create prototypes that drive the product vision forward.
Propose impactful ideas to enhance user experience by analyzing and anticipating user needs, actively collaborating with others to solve strategic, product, or technological constraints.

Proficiency in data analysis, synthesis, and planning.

High level of craftsmanship in design execution.
