The team develops industry-leading vehicle accessories and interior systems that enhance luxury, craftsmanship, functionality, ergonomics, performance, and overall customer experience.
This engineer will support the design, development, validation, and launch of accessory products across interior, exterior, performance, and lifestyle categories, from concept through production release.
•Design and develop automotive accessory components and systems.
•Create CAD models, engineering drawings, DVP&R documentation, DFMEAs, validation plans, and engineering releases.
•Collaborate with Design, Manufacturing, Quality, Purchasing, Marketing, Program Management, and suppliers.
•Lead supplier reviews, feasibility assessments, sourcing support, and tooling discussions.
•Support prototype builds, validation testing, and production launches.
•Drive issue resolution, cost optimization, and continuous improvement efforts.
•Mentor junior engineers as needed.
This role offers complete ownership of accessory development programs from concept through production. The engineer will have the opportunity to influence customer-facing products and directly see their work integrated into Lucid vehicles.
•End-to-end product ownership
•Direct impact on customer experience and vehicle personalization
•Collaboration with Design Studio and cross-functional vehicle teams
•Opportunity to work on premium luxury automotive products
•Exposure to a wide variety of materials, suppliers, and manufacturing processes
•Creative freedom and innovation-focused environment
