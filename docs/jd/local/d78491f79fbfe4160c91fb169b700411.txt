About This Role
Zof AI is seeking a User Experience Designer to design how engineers come to trust autonomous agents that change their code. This role owns the research and interaction work behind the review surface: reading the evidence behind a proposed fix, judging whether the validation holds, and approving it or sending it back. If you have worked as a Product Designer, Interaction Designer, UX Researcher, or UI/UX Designer, this is that discipline at Zof AI. The ideal candidate talks to real engineers, turns those conversations into flows and interaction patterns, and treats the design system as how good decisions stay consistent.
Design the review experience where engineers accept, reject, or revise agent-proposed code changes.
Run research with engineers and QA leads to learn how trust in automation is built and lost.
Design how evidence, reproduction steps, and validation results are presented so review decisions are fast and informed.
Turn research findings into flows, wireframes, and interaction specs engineering can build from.
Prototype and test interaction patterns for agent status, uncertainty, and failure states.
Extend the design system with components and patterns for reviewing machine-generated work.
Partner with product and engineering from problem framing through shipped experience.
Use AI tools daily to compress the loop from research question to testable prototype.
