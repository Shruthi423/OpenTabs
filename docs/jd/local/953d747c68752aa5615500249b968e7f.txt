Job Description
Assignment Dates: September 14, 2026 – September 12, 2027
We are seeking an experienced User Experience Designer to help shape and scale a design system supporting digital products across web and mobile platforms. In this role, you will create reusable design foundations, improve component consistency, and enable product teams to deliver accessible, high-quality experiences more efficiently.
You will work closely with product designers, engineers, product leaders, and accessibility partners to translate shared design patterns into practical, well-documented solutions. This opportunity is ideal for a systems-minded designer who has actively contributed to building, evolving, or governing a design system.
Design and refine reusable UI components, interaction patterns, and shared assets in Figma.
Develop scalable design solutions that improve consistency across product teams and digital channels.
Maintain component libraries, variants, auto-layout structures, and shared design resources.
Create clear documentation, usage guidance, standards, and best practices for design-system adoption.
Partner with engineering teams to align design components with production implementations.
Identify and resolve gaps between design artifacts, coded components, and user experience standards.
Apply responsive, accessible, and inclusive design principles to reusable patterns and components.
Participate in design critiques, contribution reviews, governance activities, and release planning.
Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to prioritize system improvements and adoption opportunities.
Evaluate emerging tools and workflows that improve design efficiency and design-to-development collaboration.
