Job Title: Global UX Research & Design Specialist
Reports to: Manager, Global UX and Optimization
Interested applicants must reside in one of the following approved states: Arizona, California, Colorado, Minnesota, New Jersey, Texas
The Role
As the Global UX Research & Design Specialist, you will be a key driver in shaping the digital experience for Deckers’ global brands. You’ll join a team dedicated to creating meaningful consumer value and business impact by delivering seamless, high-performing experiences across digital and physical touchpoints. Your work will elevate brand engagement, optimize performance, and enable scalable growth through insight-driven strategies, innovative design, and operational excellence. This role is meaningful because you will transform the way millions of consumers interact with our brands, making every digital journey intuitive, engaging, and impactful.
We celebrate diversity--of your background, your experiences and your unique identity. We are committed to ensuring an inclusive and equitable workplace where all of our employees can Come as They Are. We believe that when we bring our different perspectives to work, we are truly Better Together.
Your Impact
Drive the transformation of Deckers’ digital experiences by uncovering deep user insights and translating them into innovative, intuitive solutions that delight millions of consumers worldwide.
Lead the charge in creating seamless, high-performing journeys across web and mobile platforms, ensuring every interaction reflects our brand’s commitment to excellence and authenticity.
Empower cross-functional teams by communicating research findings and actionable recommendations that spark alignment, inspire creativity, and accelerate business growth.
Champion experimentation and optimization, leveraging behavioral analytics and A/B testing to continuously refine and elevate the consumer experience.
Shape the future of global UX by designing scalable systems, fostering collaboration, and setting new standards for accessibility, inclusivity, and performance.
Be a catalyst for meaningful change—your work will directly impact how Deckers connects with customers, drives engagement, and achieves business success in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.
Who You Are
Strong human-centered mindset with experience in site optimization, research, testing, design, and analytics.
Skilled in leading research and design strategies that meet user needs across the globe.
Experienced in creating a data-driven and collaborative decision-making environment.
Advanced industry knowledge of innovative technology and UX/UI practices.
Proficient in optimization and UX tools, with strong attention to detail and advanced data management skills.
Experienced with Figma and familiar with HTML/CSS/JS and visual editors.
Bachelor’s degree in UX, User Centered Design preferred and/or related work experience.
5+ years of experience in user research, UX design, digital design, digital analytics, A/B testing, personalization platforms, and behavioral analytics (Fullstory, Content Square).
Demonstrates Deckers global leadership competencies: authenticity, championing diverse teams, driving strategy, cultivating innovation, delivering results, and building connection.
