As a Senior UX Researcher, you will support two product squads by planning and executing qualitative and mixedmethods research, including interviews, usability testing, and survey-based studies. You will partner closely with Product Managers, UX Designers, UX Strategists, and Engineering Leads to uncover user needs, generate actionable insights, and influence product decisions. This role focuses on products supporting nursing education and assessments and requires collaboration with a global UX Research team across multiple regions.
The ideal candidate can independently manage multiple research initiatives, communicate findings effectively to stakeholders, and advocate for the user throughout the product development lifecycle.
This role is remote but candidates must sit in the Greater Philadelphia/ New Jersey area. Compensation ranges from $50-57/hr.
