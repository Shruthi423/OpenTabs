We’re looking for a UX Product Designer to help create intuitive, user-friendly experiences for native iOS and Android applications. You’ll work closely with Product, Engineering, and business teams in an Agile environment.
What You’ll Do
Design user experiences, interfaces, workflows, and interactions.
Create user flows, wireframes, mockups, and prototypes.
Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing.
Use research and UX best practices to influence product decisions.
Collaborate with developers from design through implementation.
Gather feedback and quickly iterate on designs.
Create and maintain UX documentation and design patterns.
Present designs and recommendations to stakeholders.
Manage multiple projects and communicate project status, risks, and issues.
Hands-on experience with native iOS and Android — required.
Strong experience with Figma, Sketch, Axure, Adobe CC, or similar tools.
Experience with wireframing, prototyping, user research, and usability testing.
Strong understanding of user-centered design and accessibility.
Experience working with developers and cross-functional teams in an Agile environment.
Strong communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
Bachelor’s degree in UX, HCI, Design, Computer Science, or related field preferred.
