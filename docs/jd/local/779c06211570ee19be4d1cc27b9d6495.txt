About the Role
We are looking for an experienced UI/UX Designer to design intuitive, scalable, and user-friendly experiences for enterprise applications.
The ideal candidate will have strong experience in enterprise UX, data-intensive applications, dashboards, workflows, information architecture, interaction design, wireframing, prototyping, and high-fidelity UI design . The role requires close collaboration with business stakeholders, Business Analysts, architects, developers, and product teams to translate complex business requirements into simple and effective user experiences.
Design intuitive user journeys, workflows, and end-to-end experiences for enterprise applications.
Translate business and user requirements into wireframes, prototypes, user flows, and high-fidelity UI designs .
Design data-rich dashboards, reports, and data visualization experiences that make complex information easy to understand.
Create effective filtering, search, drill-down, navigation, and information discovery experiences.
Develop and maintain reusable design patterns, components, and UI standards to ensure consistency across applications.
Work closely with business stakeholders, Business Analysts, architects, developers, and product teams throughout the design and development lifecycle.
Conduct and incorporate user feedback, usability considerations, and design validation into solutions.
Support developers during implementation by providing design specifications, assets, and design clarification .
Ensure consistency, accessibility, usability, and visual quality across application screens and workflows.
Identify usability issues and recommend improvements to existing enterprise applications.
Contribute to the evolution of design systems and UX standards across products.
