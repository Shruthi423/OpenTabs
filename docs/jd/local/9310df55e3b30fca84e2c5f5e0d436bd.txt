You’ll Partner Closely With Product Designers, Product Managers, Engineers, Operations, Data Scientists, And Policy Experts To Build Solutions That Balance Safety, Speed, And User Trust. This Could Include Designing Appeals Flows, Account Standing And Compliance Experiences, Internal Review And Enforcement Tools, And Real-time Interventions During Live Shows. What You’ll Do
Design end-to-end experiences that protect marketplace integrity while minimizing friction for legitimate users
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate complex policies, workflows, and risk signals into intuitive product experiences
Create wireframes, prototypes, and polished visuals that bring clarity to ambiguous Trust & Risk problems
Design experiences that make enforcement decisions feel clear, fair, human, and actionable
Support internal teams who review reports, moderate content, investigate fraud, and resolve user issues through efficient, usable tooling
Contribute to the broader design system while advocating for accessibility and inclusive user experiences
Use data, research, and feedback to improve existing tools and identify new opportunities to reduce harm and build user trust
