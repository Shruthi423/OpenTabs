We are looking for a visionary and hands-on creative leader to significantly shape the visual identity and narrative of a diverse portfolio of brands. This is an incredible opportunity to drive design strategy, execute high-impact creative solutions, and ensure brand cohesion across all platforms. Your expertise will directly influence how this forward-thinking company communicates its mission and connects with its audience, making a tangible difference in their impactful work.
What You Will Do
Lead design strategy and direction for all programs, products, and key moments.
Actively participate in marketing, brand, and design discussions, providing valuable feedback during planning and strategy meetings.
Proactively take ownership of each project, from initial brainstorms to final production, with minimal instruction.
Successfully manage project requests from various stakeholders, balancing workload to meet all deadlines.
Utilize strong communication and organizational skills to define project scope, vision, deliverables, key messaging, and necessary assets.
Quickly translate ideas and moodboards into sketches, wireframes, mockups, and prototypes, leveraging tools like Adobe Creative Suite and Figma.
Manage relationships with external agencies engaged for specific projects.
Ensure consistency in designs across all company deliverables.
Participate in creative reviews with team members, incorporating recommended changes into final designs.
Must-Have Qualifications
Demonstrated experience in the design thinking process.
Proven ability and flexibility to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
Experience as a project manager and/or creative lead.
Expertise with standard design tools, including Adobe Creative Suite and Figma.
Strong knowledge of current digital design best practices.
A solid understanding of visual design principles, coupled with experience in user-centered design.
A strong portfolio showcasing the breadth of your design capabilities.
Strong work ethic and personal accountability.
Adaptability and flexibility to meet the changing needs of the business.
A self-starter and effective problem solver who thrives in ambiguous and fast-paced environments.
Ability to clearly communicate conceptual ideas and an openness to feedback.
Excellent interpersonal skills and the ability to build strong working relationships.
Aquent Studios is a global creative studio that delivers scale, speed and efficiency to the world’s most recognized brands. We create the work that matters the most to them—work that has a real impact on their business. As an Aquent Studios team member, you’ll have the opportunity to engage with the largest and most influential companies in the world, solving design challenges and extending their brands across tactic types and platforms, including digital design, UI/UX, video, motion, content, print design, web development, and more. If you want to do work that matters in a collaborative setting that values ingenuity and personal growth, you have found the right place!
