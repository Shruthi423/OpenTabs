You'll contribute to design critiques, collaborate on user testing, and help refine our design processes. This role is ideal for designers ready to deepen their craft and take on more responsibility in shaping product experiences.
Translate business requirements and user insights into intuitive, user-centered design solutions across desktop and mobile platforms.
Assist in user research activities, including persona development, heuristic evaluations, usability reviews, and competitive analysis, to inform design decisions.
Create and iterate on UX deliverables such as user flows, journey maps, wireframes, mockups, prototypes (low/high fidelity), and information architecture.

Collaborate with cross-functional teams-including product managers, developers, content strategists, and UX leads-to align on goals, constraints, and implementation strategies.

Participate in agile ceremonies and workflows, providing design assets, specifications, and feedback throughout the development lifecycle.
Support usability testing and review behavioral analytics to validate and optimize design performance.
Maintain detailed design specifications and partner with developers to ensure accurate implementation, including involvement in user acceptance testing.
Stay current with UX trends, tools, and technologies, and contribute to evolving team processes and design practices.
