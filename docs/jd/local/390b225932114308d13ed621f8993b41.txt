As a member of the Indexable Milling Product Engineering team, the Engineer is responsible for planning and executing new product development projects independently, leading global product development initiatives, and providing engineering support across multiple project teams. The role establishes technical and functional product requirements, conducts experimental analysis to validate designs, and collaborates with Product Management, Field Sales, Customers, Corporate Technology, and Manufacturing to develop and implement new products. The Engineer is also responsible for the maintenance and expansion of existing product lines.
New Product Development – Lead and execute new product development projects from concept through commercialization.
Project Leadership – Plan, prioritize, and coordinate engineering activities while delegating responsibilities as required to achieve project objectives.
Technical Development – Establish technical and functional requirements for products based on engineering expertise and market needs.
Design Validation – Conduct experimental testing and analysis to validate product designs and performance requirements.
Cross-Functional Collaboration – Interface with Product Management, Field Sales, Customers, Corporate Technology, and Manufacturing to support new product implementation.
Product Line Support – Maintain and expand existing product lines through continuous improvement and engineering support.
