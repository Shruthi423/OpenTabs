Role Description The Product Design Intern will work directly with the founders and engineering team to shape how users interact with Complication. You’ll help design product flows, wireframes, prototypes, and polished interfaces for complex professional workflows. You may work on ways for users to move between documents, facts, decisions, relationships, and deadlines, or turn feedback from design partners into simpler and more intuitive product experiences. You’ll also contribute to usability testing, user research, our visual system, and reusable design components. Because we are an early-stage company, you’ll have significant ownership and the opportunity to influence the product from the beginning. The role is based in Berkeley, CA, with flexibility for remote work.
Qualifications
Strong foundation in Product Design and Design Thinking, with the ability to translate complex concepts into intuitive user experiences.
Skills in User Research and UX Research, including gathering insights from users and contributing to usability testing and analysis.
Proficiency in Visual Design, including layout, typography, and basic interaction design using modern design tools (e.g., Figma, Sketch, or similar).
Currently enrolled in or recently graduated from a college or university; students from any field of study are welcome to apply.
Ability to work collaboratively in a multidisciplinary team, receive and apply feedback, and communicate design decisions clearly.
Interest in complex systems, AI-enabled products, and the intersection of human expertise and automated workflows.
Organized, self-directed, and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with evolving requirements. UC Berkeley students are especially encouraged to apply.
