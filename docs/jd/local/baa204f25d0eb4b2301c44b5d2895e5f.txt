Picasso is a focused, design-driven lighting company with big ambitions. We’re small, agile, hands-on and growing quickly. This is a chance to join early and play a real role in shaping our products, processes, and direction. As we scale, your impact will grow with us.
Working alongside an experienced leadership team, you’ll be a key member of the Picasso Design & Engineering group, developing high-performance luminaires from concept through production. This role blends strong design instincts with solid mechanical understanding, ideal for someone who enjoys taking ownership and turning ideas into real, manufacturable products.
In our fast-moving environment, you’ll be involved across the full product lifecycle: research, sketching, CAD modeling, prototyping, testing, and launch. You’ll help shape not just the products, but how we build them.
Working closely with Senior Leadership, Engineering, Production, Sales, and Marketing
Lead designer on both new and custom product development
Develops designs through sketches, mock-ups, prototypes, and detailed 3D CAD models
Applies Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA) principles
Creates technical drawings, assemblies, and bills of materials
Supports prototyping, testing, validation, and risk analysis
Assists with product launches, renderings, and documentation
Helps build scalable product development processes as we grow
Degree in Industrial Design, Mechanical Engineering, or related field
3+ years of experience developing architectural lighting products
Proficiency in Inventor or SolidWorks, Key Shot (or similar), AutoCAD, and Adobe/Microsoft Suites
Good understanding of manufacturing processes (sheet metal, extrusion, die casting, CNC, 3D printing)
Knowledge of LED, drivers, controls, or lighting performance testing is an asset
Self-starter with strong design judgment and attention to proportion
Practical, organized, and comfortable in a fast-paced setting
Team-oriented with clear communication skills
Motivated to grow into greater responsibility as the company expands
