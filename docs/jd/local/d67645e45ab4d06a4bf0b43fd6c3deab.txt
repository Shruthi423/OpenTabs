About The Role
We're hiring a Technical Product Designer to join a small, high-impact design team. This is a Product Design role first — you'll own real feature work end to end, from concept through shipped experience. What makes it different: you're comfortable enough with front-end code (React, component-based systems) to partner directly with engineering on our design system, and help contribute to our "design-to-code" workflows.
You won't be asked to ship production features solo — you'll be a design-side technical partner who helps close the gap between what we design and what we build.
What You'll Do
Own product design work for one of our Pod's — partnering with Product and Engineering on discovery, concepting, prototyping, and testing.
Help strengthen and extend our design system alongside another designer, including contributing directly by designing and potentially implementing components where useful
Pair with Head of Design to explore design infrastructure that makes it easier for Design, Product, and Engineering to stay in sync (e.g., AI-enabled design workflows, design-to-code processes)
Help set up the Design team to pick up frontend-adjacent work that often falls between design and engineering — polish, small UI fixes that rarely get prioritized, prototyping in code
Participate in customer calls and research alongside the rest of the Design team
Requirements
3+ years as a product/UX designer or design engineer, with a portfolio showing real shipped product work, not just concepts
Comfortable reading and writing basic front-end code (HTML/CSS/JS or React) — enough to prototype, contribute to a design system, or have a real conversation with engineers about implementation
Strong craft fundamentals: interaction design, visual design, and the judgment to know when detail matters and when it doesn't
Self-sufficient and comfortable in a small team where you'll wear a few hats
Experience working directly with a design system or component library team
Experience with AI-assisted design/developer tooling (i.e. MCPs, design-harnesses, etc.)
Experience at a B2B SaaS or vertical software company
Paid Time Off (Vacation, Sick & Public Holidays)
