UI/UX Designer at Surge: Crafting Intuitive and Aesthetic User Experiences
Surge is seeking a creative and skilled UI/UX Designer to join our dynamic team. This role involves using your expertise in design tools like Adobe XD, Sketch, and Figma to develop intuitive and aesthetically pleasing user interfaces for both web and mobile applications. At Surge, you will play a crucial role in ensuring our products are not only functional but also provide an engaging user experience. Join us to contribute to our mission of delivering innovative and user-centered design solutions.
At Surge, we're committed to innovation in technology and user experience. Our culture is one where creativity, collaboration, and technical excellence are at the forefront. Joining Surge means being part of a team that values your expertise in UI/UX design and encourages you to push the boundaries of creativity. We believe in creating products that not only meet user needs but also exceed their expectations in aesthetics and functionality.
Role of UI/UX Designer at Surge: Design Tools Mastery, Collaborative Development
As a UI/UX Designer at Surge, your responsibilities will include:
Developing and conceptualizing a comprehensive UI/UX design strategy for the brand.
Producing high-quality UX design solutions through wireframes, visual and graphic designs, flow diagrams, storyboards, site maps, and prototypes.
Collaborating with our product team and developers to ensure the creation of engaging, intuitive, and user-friendly interfaces.
Testing UI elements such as CTAs, layouts, target links, and landing pages.
Implementing responsive design to ensure a consistent, intuitive experience across all devices.
Essential Skills for Surge’s UI/UX Designer: Design Principles, Adobe XD, Sketch
Proficiency in UI/UX design software such as Adobe XD, Sketch, and Figma.
Strong understanding of UI/UX design principles and methodologies, with a portfolio showcasing previous work.
Experience in creating wireframes, user flows, process flows, and site maps.
Familiarity with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and responsive web design.
Strong problem-solving skills and a collaborative nature, are essential for working with cross-functional teams.
Advanced UI/UX Development Skills at Surge: User Research, Prototyping
Experience in conducting user research and usability testing to guide design decisions.
Proficiency in prototyping tools and knowledge of current mobile and web design trends.
Ability to receive constructive feedback and integrate it into design solutions.
Surge offers a competitive salary range of $70,000 to $100,000, based on experience and proficiency in UI/UX design. Our dynamic work environment fosters professional growth and development, offering opportunities for you to advance your skills and career.
