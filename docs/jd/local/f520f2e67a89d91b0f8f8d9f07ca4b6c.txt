This role serves as the bridge between advanced complex cybersecurity capabilities and the people rely on it. By transforming technical workflows into clear, intuitive experiences, the Product Designer ensures that users can act quickly, make informed decisions, and trust the tools at their disposal. Strong design here isn’t just aesthetic—it is critical to fostering confidence, adoption, and effectiveness in safeguarding our customers. In a field where confusion can mean vulnerability, thoughtful design directly strengthens security outcomes.
What You’ll Do
Drive end-to-end Product design for cybersecurity products, from research and ideation to wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs in Figma.
Translate complex security concepts into clear, user-friendly workflows that enable users to respond effectively.
Collaborate with engineers and security experts to ensure design solutions are both usable and technically feasible.
Build and maintain a scalable design system that drives consistency and accessibility across products.
Advocate for the user’s perspective in every stage of the product lifecycle, ensuring our solutions serve people as effectively as they provide security.
5–10 years of professional experience as a UX Designer, Interaction Designer, or Product Designer.
Strong proficiency in Figma, Miro or Sketch for wireframing, prototyping, and creating design systems.
Demonstrated experience working on complex digital products (web and/or large screens).
Background in designing for global audiences and accessibility standards (WCAG).
Excellent understanding of user-centered design principles, accessibility, and responsive design.
Strong collaboration and communication skills to work effectively with cross-functional teams.
Bonus: Experience collaborating with Cybersecurity teams.
NOTE: Please be sure to include a link to your portfolio either on your resume or in the website link field, and include your portfolio password if necessary to access it on your resume. Applicants without a portfolio will not be considered.
Work Location – San Jose, CA (5 days in the office)
