In this role, you'll own critical design decisions, apply advanced engineering principles, and work directly with customers, sales, manufacturing, and technology experts to develop products that create competitive advantage in rapidly evolving markets.
If you enjoy turning challenging technical problems into manufacturable solutions and seeing your designs move from concept to production, we'd like to talk with you.
What You'll Do
Lead the design and development of innovative interconnect products from concept through production launch.
Drive technical solutions for connectors, cable assemblies, high-speed systems, power products, and emerging technologies.
Partner with Marketing and Sales to evaluate new opportunities and translate customer needs into engineering solutions.
Collaborate with manufacturing, quality, test, process engineering, suppliers, and other technical experts to ensure successful product commercialization.
Perform design analysis, modeling, validation, and troubleshooting to optimize product performance, reliability, and manufacturability.
Influence product architecture, design standards, and engineering best practices.
Solve complex technical challenges during product development and production support activities.
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives that enhance product quality, performance, and cost competitiveness.
Why This Role Appeals To Experienced Engineers
Significant influence on product design and technical direction.
Exposure to a broad portfolio of advanced interconnect technologies.
Opportunity to work on challenging high-speed, power, and electromechanical applications.
Direct collaboration with cross-functional teams throughout the entire product lifecycle.
Ability to see your designs move from concept to production and customer deployment.
Opportunity to mentor engineers and contribute to engineering excellence.
7–10+ years of experience designing and developing interconnect, electromechanical, connector, or cable products.
Strong CAD design experience using SolidWorks, AutoCAD, or equivalent platforms.
Proven track record of bringing products from concept through production.
Ability to manage multiple technical projects while balancing innovation, performance, cost, and manufacturability.
Excellent communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills.
Preferred Qualifications
High-speed connector design and development experience.
Signal integrity, power integrity, or high-speed system design knowledge.
Experience with simulation and analysis tools including FEA, CFD, thermal modeling, or similar technologies.
Experience working closely with manufacturing processes and production environments.
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
Appliances, Electrical, and Electronics Manufacturing
