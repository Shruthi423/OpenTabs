You’ll bring exceptional attention to detail, a love of experimentation and a high bar for visual craft. At Pinterest, AI isn’t just a feature—it’s a powerful partner that augments our creativity and amplifies our impact. We’re looking for candidates who are excited to explore how AI can strengthen their work while applying sound judgment and ownership to the final outcome.
What You’ll Do
Design and ship notification experiences across push, email and in-app surfaces on iOS, Android, desktop and web.
Create clear, cohesive journeys spanning notification permissions, delivery, entry points, preferences and settings.
Translate ambiguous product problems into clear design requirements, user flows and interaction patterns.
Partner closely with product managers, engineers, researchers and data scientists to identify opportunities, design experiments and improve engagement outcomes.
Use qualitative and quantitative insights to refine visual hierarchy, messaging, micro-interactions and the timing of notification experiences.
Create high-fidelity designs, prototypes and detailed specifications that build on and extend the Pinterest design system.
Clearly communicate design decisions, tradeoffs and recommendations, advocating for user-centered solutions that support product and business goals.
Use AI-enabled workflows to accelerate exploration, prototyping and synthesis while validating quality, accessibility and Pinner impact.
Share progress early, address blockers proactively and contribute to a respectful, collaborative working environment.
2+ years of product design experience, ideally in growth, engagement or consumer-facing mobile products.
A portfolio that demonstrates strong product and visual design craft, including end-to-end flows for mobile and web.
Experience designing, shipping and iterating on consumer product experiences in close partnership with product management and engineering.
Strong understanding of UX principles and the ability to translate business goals into testable hypotheses and experiments.
A data-informed approach, with experience using research, experimentation and product metrics to improve designs.
Ability to scope and drive projects in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment.
Mastery of Figma, solid prototyping skills and experience producing clear handoff documentation for engineering.
Thoughtful handling of edge cases, accessibility and user control in cross-channel experiences.
Clear communication skills and a collaborative, low-ego approach to working across functions.
Bachelor’s degree in design, a related field or equivalent experience.
