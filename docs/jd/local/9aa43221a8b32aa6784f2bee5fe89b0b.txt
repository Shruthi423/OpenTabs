As the Associate Product Designer for Pokémon Plastics, you will play a key role in designing and developing innovative, high-quality plastic toys, action figures, and playsets for one of our major licensed properties. Combining creative ideation, a deep appreciation for the Pokémon universe, and technical hard-goods problem-solving, you will turn fan-favorite Pokémon, Trainers, and battle environments into compelling physical toys that resonate with collectors and kids alike. Come join one of the world's biggest toy-making companies and help us shape the future of play!
What You Will Do:
Participate in the entire product development lifecycle—from initial ideation and concept generation through refinement, prototype execution, and final turnover packages
Collaborate cross-functionally with brand, product management, engineering, packaging, and licensors to ensure designs align with overarching strategies and target costs
Create precise, clear, and complete product turnover specifications and ensure they are fully understood by global international teams and factory partners
Review, document, and submit specification changes for licensor approval while providing clear feedback on illustrative turns, sculpts, paint masters, and sample reviews
Attend brand and licensing brainstorming sessions, successfully presenting new toy concepts and designs to management and licensor teams
Champion high-quality design standards and work closely with management to resolve development bottlenecks while assuring products meet strict QA/QC requirements
1 to 3 years of experience in product design, specifically focused on hard goods, plastic toy development, or action figures
1 to 3 years of experience collaborating with overseas manufacturing partners (Asia preferred)
Genuine passion for the Pokémon universe, characters, region lore, and aesthetics, with a strong pulse on what the collector and kid communities look for in plastic figures and playsets
Strong proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign) and computer proficiency in Microsoft Office and G-Suite
Knowledge of materials, multiple manufacturing processes, and current trends or innovations in the competitive toy marketplace
Exceptional visual, verbal, and written communication skills with a demonstrated ability to present concepts and build consensus across departments
A proactive problem-solver and critical thinker who can adapt to shifting priorities, manage multiple tasks efficiently, and maintain excellent attention to detail
Bachelor’s degree in Toy Design, Industrial Design, Industrial Engineering, or an industry-relevant field is a plus
