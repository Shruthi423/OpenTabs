Position Summary
We are seeking a creative and customer-focused User Interface Designer / Infotainment UX Engineer to develop intuitive, engaging, and high-quality infotainment experiences. In this role, you will leverage user research, competitive benchmarking, and UX design methodologies to define and deliver innovative infotainment features and interfaces that meet customer needs and support business objectives.
Conduct and apply user-centered research to understand customer behaviors, needs, and pain points.
Design intuitive infotainment user experiences, workflows, wireframes, and interface concepts.
Develop and validate design solutions through prototyping, usability feedback, and iterative improvements.
Perform competitive product benchmarking and identify opportunities to enhance user experience.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including engineering, product development, Human Factors, and design stakeholders.
Create and present design concepts, research findings, and recommendations to leadership and project teams.
Ensure designs meet usability, quality, and business requirements.
Required Qualifications
3+ years of experience in UX Design, UI Design, Human Factors, Product Development, or a related field.
Experience conducting user-centered research in academic or professional environments.
Product benchmarking and competitive analysis experience.
Proficiency with UX/UI and prototyping tools such as Figma, ProtoPie, Axure, Kanzi , or similar platforms.
Strong understanding of user-centered design principles and usability methodologies.
Excellent verbal, written, and presentation skills.
Ability to translate research insights into actionable design solutions.
