Qualifications:
Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, Analytics, or a related field.
1-3 years of experience in digital marketing, e-commerce, customer experience, conversion optimization, or a related role.
Experience with tools such as GA4, Shopify, Looker Studio, Optimizely, or similar platforms.
Strong analytical, project management, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills.
Ability to turn customer and performance data into clear, actionable recommendations.
Strong attention to detail and proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets.
Experience in fashion, retail, bridal, or consumer e-commerce is preferred.
Experience with CRM, personalization, or customer support is preferred.
Hands-on experience using generative AI or automation tools is highly preferred.
100% Medical, Dental, Vision, Life insurance offered after a 60-day probationary period
