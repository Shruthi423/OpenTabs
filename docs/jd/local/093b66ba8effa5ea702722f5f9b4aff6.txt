Our Product Design (Experience Design / XD) team partners closely with Product Managers, Engineers, Analysts, and Business leaders across Wayfair to ensure we're building technology that solves important customer problems in elegant, scalable, and measurable ways—across all platforms, stores, and geographies.
About The Role
As a Product Designer II (L2) on the Financing & Loyalty team, you will help design the core engine of Wayfair’s customer retention ecosystem—bringing together Wayfair Credit Card (WFCC) and Wayfair Rewards (WFR).
In this high-visibility role, you will be a core executor focusing on crafting seamlessly integrated, end-to-end user journeys for our brand-new credit card experience (from applications and account management to rewards redemption). Working under the guidance and mentorship of a Senior Design Lead, you will bridge the gap between our loyalty and financing touchpoints, ensuring every interaction across the shopping funnel feels intuitive, transparent, and cohesive.
What You’ll Do
Drive Tactical Execution & Craft: Deliver high-polish, consumer-grade UI and interaction design across mobile apps and responsive web for complex, multi-step customer flows.
Build Ecosystem Cohesion: Design tailored tactical touchpoints (such as credit card application flows and rewards dashboards) while ensuring they integrate naturally into the broader shopping funnel and Loyalty framework.
Manage Complex Edge Cases: Solve nuanced design challenges by accounting for financial logic, contractual obligations, third-party vendor integrations, and various customer state transitions within a rewards ecosystem.
Establish User Trust & Privacy: Design secure, clear, and transparent experiences that build customer confidence when interacting with sensitive personal and financial information (PII and credit applications).
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Product Management, Engineering, and commercial stakeholders to translate complex requirements into scalable, technically feasible solutions.
Leverage & Contribute to Systems: Utilize Figma components, variants, and our internal design system to maintain UI polish, high-fidelity prototypes, and clear, efficient developer handoffs.
2+ years of Product Design experience with a portfolio showcasing strong craft, end-to-end execution, and thoughtful UI/UX solutions for responsive web and mobile apps.
Deep fluency in Figma, UI components/variants, rapid prototyping, and design systems practices.
Detail-oriented problem solving with a focus on edge cases, user state management, and clear information hierarchy.
Strong communication skills to articulate design choices, present work effectively, and collaborate cross-functionally.
E-Commerce, Loyalty, Financing, or FinTech experience (e.g., Cart, Checkout, Credit Cards, or Rewards programs) is highly preferred.
Understanding of trust and transparency design principles when handling sensitive customer or financial data.
Bachelor’s degree in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Interaction Design, Graphic Design, or equivalent practical experience.
