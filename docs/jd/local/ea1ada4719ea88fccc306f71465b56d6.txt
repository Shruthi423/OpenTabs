Are you a creative and curious designer eager to make a profound impact? This is an exceptional opportunity for a **Visual Designer** to transform innovative ideas into compelling brand experiences that reach millions. You will play a pivotal role in shaping the visual identity of a globally recognized brand, contributing to high-profile campaigns and web experiences that will be seen by a vast audience. This position offers an early-career professional the chance to apply strong creative instincts, explore new ideas, and continually elevate the quality of work within a dynamic and supportive environment, truly influencing how a global brand communicates visually.
What You’ll Do
Create thoughtful visual concepts for digital marketing campaigns, web experiences, and brand initiatives.
Translate brand strategy into engaging creative that is distinctive, consistent, and effective.
Explore multiple creative directions, iterate quickly, and present clear design rationale.
Collaborate with writers, strategists, researchers, developers, and marketers across a global organization to bring visions to life.
Contribute to the evolution of the visual language and design system, leaving your mark on the brand’s aesthetic.
Use research, accessibility best practices, and user feedback to inform creative decisions, ensuring inclusive and effective designs.
Leverage modern AI-powered creative tools to accelerate ideation, exploration, and production while applying strong design judgment.
A bachelor’s degree in graphic design, visual communication, interaction design, or a related field (or equivalent portfolio and practical experience).
A portfolio that demonstrates original thinking, strong visual craft, and an ability to solve communication challenges, not just polished execution.
An independent thinker who is comfortable working through ambiguity and proposing original ideas.
Strong visual craft, typography, layout, composition, and storytelling skills.
Proficiency with Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud.
Familiarity with design systems and collaborating effectively with front-end developers.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills to thrive in a global team environment.
Qualifications That Set You Apart
An interest in AI-assisted creative workflows and emerging design technologies.
Experience in B2B technology or at a creative agency.
A proactive approach, consistently asking “What if?” before “What’s next?”.
A mindset focused on generating ideas, not just delivering files.
The ability to balance creativity with business objectives, ensuring designs meet strategic goals.
A commitment to valuing feedback and using it constructively to improve your work.
A passion for accessibility, inclusion, and creating simple, memorable experiences for all users.
A continuous learner who enjoys exploring new tools and refining their craft.
