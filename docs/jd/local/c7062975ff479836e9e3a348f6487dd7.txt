About The Role
The role owns the end-to-end design process for core product experiences, transforming complex technical constraints into intuitive, human-centered interfaces used by millions.
The design team collaborates closely with product managers and software engineers to shape how products look, feel, and function, championing user research and rigorous prototyping.
Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing to identify user pain points and validate design hypotheses
Create wireframes, user flows, low-fidelity prototypes, and high-fidelity UI designs using Figma and design systems
Collaborate with frontend engineers to ensure design integrity, accessibility standards (WCAG), and smooth implementation
Design and maintain a scalable component-based design system to accelerate product development cycles
Present design concepts, research findings, and strategic decisions to cross-functional stakeholders and leadership
Analyze post-launch product metrics and user feedback to iterate on existing features and identify new opportunities
3–6 years of product design experience focused on digital web and mobile applications
Mastery of modern design and prototyping tools, specifically Figma, with a strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end UX/UI execution
Deep understanding of user-centered design methodologies, interaction design principles, and information architecture
Experience planning and conducting qualitative and quantitative user research studies
Bachelor's degree in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Graphic Design, Industrial Design, or equivalent practical experience
Bonus: Experience designing data-dense dashboards, working with complex SaaS workflows, or basic frontend coding skills in HTML/CSS/React
