This is an early-career role for someone eager to grow quickly in a technically demanding environment. You'll work alongside experienced engineers and designers who will support your development through mentorship, peer feedback, and collaborative critique. As you ramp up, you'll take on real feature work — conceiving UI components, reimagining existing workflows, and coordinating closely with developers on implementation. The problems we work on are complex, and you will be supported by your team and leadership to engage with and grow into that complexity over time.
Prior Space-industry Experience Is Not Required.
Translate customer and operator requirements into clean, intuitive designs
Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes that work within and contribute to an existing design system
Collaborate with designers, product managers, developers, and mission operators to refine requirements and incorporate feedback
Maintain and organize design files and document design decisions
Participate in agile ceremonies including sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
Required Qualifications
1-2 years of UI/UX design experience, including internships and academic or personal projects
Proficiency in Figma, including components, auto-layout, and design system workflows
Experience creating wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
Experience defining user flows and identifying friction points
Strong verbal and written communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to work in a collaborative team environment
Ability to proactively seek information and guidance when encountering vague or confusing requirements
Ability to obtain and hold a security clearance
Design portfolio demonstrating UI/UX work from previous projects, internships, or academic work
Preferred Qualifications
Experience designing dark mode exclusive applications
Experience designing for colorblind accessibility
Familiarity with frontend development concepts such as component-based architecture or responsive layout
