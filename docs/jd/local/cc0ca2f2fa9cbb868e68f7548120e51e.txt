About the Role:
The Brand Designer is a junior level role supporting the development and execution of high-quality, on-brand creative across print, email, and broader marketing channels. Working closely with the Creative Director and cross-functional marketing, brand, and product partners, this role translates creative briefs into clear, effective visual solutions while helping maintain consistency across the company’s growing portfolio of brands, including Tru Niagen and Niagen Plus.
Create high-quality brand and print assets, including packaging updates, product inserts, marketing collateral, and sales materials
Maintain and support brand consistency across print, email, and broader marketing touchpoints within the brand portfolio, including Tru Niagen and Niagen Plus
Assist in evolving and applying brand guidelines, templates, and design standards across the portfolio
Prepare accurate, production-ready creative files and support quality control throughout the design and production process
Design and produce marketing and lifecycle email creative, including layout, imagery, and integration of approved copy
Adapt and optimize email templates across campaigns and platforms while considering platform requirements and responsive-design best practices
Partner with marketing stakeholders to ensure email creative is on-brand, clear, engaging, and aligned with campaign objectives
Collaborate with marketing, brand, and product teams to translate creative briefs into clear, compelling, and on-brand visual solutions
Support concept development and creative execution for campaigns, product launches, and seasonal initiatives
Work with the Creative Director and broader creative team to support integrated campaigns across multiple channels
Incorporate feedback thoughtfully and efficiently while maintaining creative quality and project timelines
Occasionally support light digital assets, including basic banners and social graphics, as needed
Perform basic photo retouching and simple compositing for print and email applications
Participate in creative brainstorming and contribute ideas that support campaign and brand objectives
Help maintain the quality, consistency, and organization of the creative team’s output and shared design assets
Qualifications and Success Factors:
1 - 3 years of professional design experience, preferably supporting wellness, supplements, health technology, consumer packaged goods, or direct-to-consumer brands
Strong design foundation, including layout, typography, composition, color, and visual hierarchy
Experience designing print materials, such as packaging, collateral, or inserts, as well as email campaign creative
Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite, particularly Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign; familiarity with Figma is a plus
Ability to work effectively within established brand systems and guidelines
Basic understanding of email-platform requirements, such as Klaviyo or Mailchimp
Strong portfolio demonstrating relevant brand, print, and email design work
Strong attention to detail and the ability to produce accurate, production-ready creative
Ability to manage multiple assignments and shifting priorities in a fast-paced environment
Preferred Qualifications:
Basic photo-retouching and compositing skills
Familiarity with light digital and social asset creation
Understanding of how creative execution supports brand consistency, customer engagement, and brand equity
Experience working in a regulated or science-forward industry, such as health, wellness, supplements, or pharmaceuticals
Produces high-quality, on-brand creative across print, email, and marketing channels with strong attention to detail
Balances creative quality with speed, responsiveness, and changing business priorities
