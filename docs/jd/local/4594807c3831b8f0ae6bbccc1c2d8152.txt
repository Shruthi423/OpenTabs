Expert Technical Solutions has an outstanding opportunity for an Associate Product Designer with an Award Winning, industry leading company in Peachtree Corners, GA. This is not a traditional UI production role. You will work with product leaders, engineers, business experts, users, and AI tools to rethink workflows from first principles. You will create interfaces and interaction models that solve difficult business problems without defaulting to the familiar patterns of legacy enterprise software.
My client is looking for a designer early in their career who prototypes quickly, learns visibly, communicates clearly, and treats AI as a normal part of the creative process.
This is a Permanent, Fully ONSITE opportunity offering competitive salary, excellent benefits, and outstanding growth opportunities!
Design modern, responsive web and mobile experiences in Figma.
Use Zeplin or similar collaboration tools to present designs, document decisions, and collect feedback through design iterations.
Use AI tools daily to explore concepts, generate alternatives, accelerate research, and prototype interaction ideas.
Create user flows, wireframes, clickable prototypes, polished interfaces, and developer-ready specifications.
Partner directly with engineers to translate designs into production experiences and resolve implementation tradeoffs.
Rethink existing enterprise workflows from first principles rather than simply reskinning current screens.
Conduct lightweight discovery, user interviews, workflow observation, and usability testing.
Iterate using feedback, product analytics, operational outcomes, and technical constraints.
Contribute to an evolving design system while recognizing when a new problem requires a new pattern.
Stay current on AI-assisted design tools, agentic workflows, conversational interaction patterns, and emerging product-design practices.

2+ years of UI/UX or product-design experience. Internships, freelance work, and strong personal projects count.
A portfolio that demonstrates thoughtful problem-solving, not only polished final screens.
Strong working proficiency in Figma, including components, variants, prototyping, and responsive layouts.
Comfort using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Lovable, v0, Bolt, Midjourney, Microsoft Copilot, or comparable tools.
Understanding of responsive design, accessibility, interaction states, and design handoff.
Clear written and verbal communication, curiosity, humility, and a willingness to learn quickly.
Comfortable working 100% on-site in Peachtree Corners, GA
