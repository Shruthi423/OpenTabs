You’ll balance strong design craft with data-informed decision-making, using user behavior and performance insights to guide UX improvements. The ideal candidate brings 5+ years of UI/UX experience, a strong portfolio showcasing thoughtful problem-solving and high-quality visual design, and advanced proficiency in Figma.
