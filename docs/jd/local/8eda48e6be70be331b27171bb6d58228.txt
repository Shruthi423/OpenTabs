This is not a role for hyper-specialists or managers. It suits someone who moves without waiting for direction and would rather build something real than hand off a mockup.
We are open to both full-time and contract-to-hire candidates, depending on experience, mutual fit, and team needs.
What You'll Do
Own features end-to-end. Lead the design process for major features, from initial product thinking and user exploration to pixel-perfect visual design and high-fidelity interactive prototypes.
Design novel interactions. Go beyond static screens. Design and prototype fluid, novel interactions for a new medium, taking complex AI concepts and making them feel intuitive and powerful.
Build functional prototypes with AI. Use AI coding tools like Cursor or Claude Code to create working prototypes and shippable features, working in close collaboration with engineering. We value designers who can make real things, not just mockups.
Uphold a high bar for craft. Create exceptionally high-quality visual designs, interactions, and prototypes, demonstrating world-class taste and an obsession with detail.
Drive work forward through ambiguity. Operate effectively in a fast-paced startup environment where direction is not always clear. Step into problems before being asked, make decisions with incomplete information, and push work forward independently.
Collaborate across disciplines. Work closely with engineers, product, and AI researchers to explore new use cases, shape product strategy, and ensure the design vision ships at high quality. Design at Luma is not a solo discipline. Outcomes are built across teams.
Who You Are
You have 5+ years of experience building delightful and innovative products at scale.
You are a strong generalist with demonstrated excellence across visual design, interaction design, and product thinking.
You integrate AI deeply into how you work. AI is visibly woven into your portfolio and your process. You can speak concretely about how AI has changed the way you design and the outcomes you produce. You have experience building functional prototypes using AI coding tools like Cursor or Claude Code.
You ship. Your portfolio demonstrates work that made it to production, not just explorations and concepts. You show the full arc: how you framed the problem, how the work evolved, and what impact it had.
You show your thinking. Your portfolio includes prototypes, iterations, and experimentation, not just polished final deliverables. You can articulate what you prioritized, what you cut, and why.
You move fast and converge. You are comfortable with rapid exploration and know when to stop iterating and commit to a direction. You balance craft with speed.
You create momentum. You have a track record of driving work forward without being asked, influencing decisions across teams, and raising the quality bar around you.
We Are Looking For Generalists, But Candidates Who Bring Depth In One Or Both Of The Following Areas Will Stand Out
Creative tools experience. You have designed for complex creative software, such as video or photo editors, design tools, or developer tools. You have a deep, intuitive understanding of creative workflows and the mindset of a creator.
World-class interaction design. Your portfolio is full of fluid, delightful micro-interactions, complex state transitions, and prototypes that go beyond static mockups. You can take abstract AI concepts and make them feel tangible and magical.
