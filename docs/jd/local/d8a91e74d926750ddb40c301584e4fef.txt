We're starting with audit and accounting workflows—high-trust environments where reliability, accuracy, and security matter as much as productivity. Our platform helps firms automate complex workflows, deploy AI safely, and modernize how audit teams operate.
The Role
We are hiring a Founding Product Designer to define how customers experience Clark.
This is a product design role focused on turning complex AI workflows into intuitive, trustworthy software. You'll work closely with Engineering, Product, Audit Engineers, and customers to understand how professionals work today and design the interfaces, workflows, and systems that make AI feel reliable in high-stakes environments.
As one of the first designers at Clark, you'll have significant ownership over our product experience—from early concepts and user research through polished production designs. You'll help establish our design language, influence product direction, and shape how customers interact with AI every day.
Clark is building software for professionals performing high-trust work.
Our users need to understand what the system is doing, why it's doing it, and where their judgment matters. Great product design isn't about making software look good—it's about making complex AI workflows feel trustworthy, fast, and intuitive.
The Founding Product Designer will help define how customers experience Clark, ensuring our products are not only powerful but approachable, transparent, and delightful to use.
What You'll Do
Design intuitive end-to-end product experiences across Clark's core platform
Partner closely with Engineering, Product, Audit Engineers, and customers to understand workflows and translate them into thoughtful product experiences
Own the design process from discovery and wireframes through high-fidelity mockups and production-ready specifications
Create intuitive workflows for AI-powered audit and accounting products
Conduct user research, gather feedback, and iterate quickly based on customer insights
Build and evolve Clark's design system, interaction patterns, and visual language
Design for clarity, trust, accessibility, and usability across complex enterprise workflows
Collaborate closely with engineers to ensure a high-quality implementation of product designs
Help establish design best practices and raise the quality bar across the organization
