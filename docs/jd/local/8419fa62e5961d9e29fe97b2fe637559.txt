This role is ideal for a designer who thinks in stories, loves the intersection of design and motion, and wants to shape how Figma's product shows up in the world, with clarity, emotion, and craft.
This is a full time role that can be held from one of our US hubs or remotely in the United States.
What you'll do at Figma:
Turn product strategy, market context, and feature complexity into storyboards and styleframes that carry the launch story
Partner with copywriters, designers, motion designers, strategists, and producers to build narrative-driven assets across launches
Help evolve Figma's visual storytelling system and apply it consistently
Own visual direction on launch storytelling projects from early concepting to final delivery
Push new visual formats and directions; don't just execute the existing playbook
A portfolio that shows strong visual point-of-view carried through to finished execution, across mediums, with examples of styleframes, storyboards, and concepting; spec work with fake brands or products is welcome
A background distilling a business or strategy brief down to a single compelling idea, and driving that idea forward into execution
Experience collaborating with motion designers, production teams, product marketing teams, and product design teams
A strong sense of composition, color, and pacing, especially in motion contexts, used in service of clarity, not abstraction
Proficiency in Figma (After Effects a plus, but not required)
While it's not required, it's an added plus if you also have:
Ability to create basic animations or motion tests independently
Interest in researching and experimenting with new tools, workflows, and technologies
Experience working on product launch films, explainer videos, or product walkthroughs
Ability to translate strategic complexity and feature detail into a story worth telling
A genuine understanding of and connection to Figma's values and mission — someone who is curious about the product and passionate about understanding it
Comfortable setting visual direction and seeing it through, driving the work autonomously when needed
At Figma, one of our values is Grow as you go. We believe in hiring smart, curious people who are excited to learn and develop their skills. If you’re excited about this role but your past experience doesn’t align perfectly with the points outlined in the job description, we encourage you to apply anyways. You may be just the right candidate for this or other roles.
