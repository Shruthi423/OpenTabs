About The Role
We're hiring our founding designer to join the small, high-output product and engineering teams at Adapt. This is an end-to-end product design role. You'll help define how companies build, run, and govern their central agent with Adapt, from customer problem through exploration, implementation, launch, and learning from real use.
We are particularly seeking a designer who cares deeply about taste, craft, and shipping, with the agency to turn ambiguous problems into clear, high-quality product experiences. You should be comfortable building a design system and prototyping or shipping in code when that's the fastest way to make progress.
