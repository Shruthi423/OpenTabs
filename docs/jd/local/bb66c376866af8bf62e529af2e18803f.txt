This is a forward-deployed role: you'll work directly with government and defense customers at key points in a project's lifecycle — discovery, feedback sessions, and post-launch — representing design in the room and building the trust needed to get stakeholder buy-in without a manager present.
Own end-to-end design — from ambiguous, undefined requirements through wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes — for a customer-facing government/defense product experience.
Partner directly with Product as a peer from the earliest stages of a project, not just at handoff.
Work forward-deployed with customers, building direct relationships during key project touch points (discovery, feedback, post-launch) and confidently socializing design decisions with stakeholders.
Design for complex, data-dense workflows: dashboards, mapping interfaces, alerting systems, and analytical reporting tools.
Work directly in code alongside engineers, going beyond static mockups to help build and refine the shipped experience.
Contribute design assets and interaction patterns back to the broader platform, not just the account or project you're staffed on.
Help calibrate and grow the design team by participating in portfolio reviews and interviews as we scale this function.
Navigate high ambiguity — customer requirements are often not clean or fully defined, and you'll need to help define them.
Influence stakeholders cross-functionally and up to leadership level to align on design direction.
Qualifications
10–15 years of experience across a range of design settings and problem types.
Experience owning end-to-end B2B or enterprise product experiences at a senior/staff level.
Comfortable working with high ambiguity and evolving requirements, typical of early-stage government/enterprise engagements.
Experience designing complex workflow UI, dashboards, or data-heavy/mapping interfaces — whether in government/defense, health tech, fintech, or other high-complexity/high-compliance domains.
Strong client-facing presence — confident presenting to and winning over senior stakeholders, whether in person or remote.
Working understanding of front-end development and how to hand off clean, implementable designs; comfort collaborating directly with engineers on code.
Willingness to travel to customer sites at key project milestones.
Experience in a client-facing role where you presented directly to senior stakeholders.
Exposure to AI/ML tools and fluency using AI in your own design workflow.
Experience contributing to a design system or shared interaction patterns across multiple products or teams.
