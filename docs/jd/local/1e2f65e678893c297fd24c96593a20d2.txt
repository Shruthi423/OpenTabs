About the role We're looking for a talented Product Designer to join our Sigma Team in Austin. You'll own design end to end, from conception to launch, on greenfield AI products used by clinical research professionals, sponsors and study teams.
On Sigma, design happens inside the build, not upstream of it. You'll sit alongside the Product Owner and AI Engineers, move from problem to demoable product in weeks rather than quarters, and use AI tools to take your own ideas as far as a working prototype before engineering picks them up. You'll conduct user research with technically sophisticated users, iterate rapidly, and help establish and evolve our design practices as the team grows.
The products you design are used to make multi-million-dollar development decisions, by people who will immediately distrust anything that feels imprecise or oversimplified. Getting the craft right here has real consequences for patients.
The Sigma Team is Biorce's Tech Special Forces, an incubator tasked with building the most disruptive, highest-stakes AI products in the clinical trial space.
This is not a team that iterates on existing products. Sigma builds from zero: defining the strategy, shipping under real speed and ambiguity, and treating every initiative as a new venture. Squads are small and cross-functional, a Product Owner, AI Engineers, an embedded Scientific Lead, and you as the designer in the room from day one, not brought in once the problem is already framed.
