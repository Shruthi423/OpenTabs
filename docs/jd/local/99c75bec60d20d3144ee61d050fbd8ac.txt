Job Description
Pangram is the AI research lab behind the world’s most accurate AI detection software. Trust and credibility are central to Pangram’s mission to be the voice of authenticity across the Internet, and Pangram’s visual identity plays a crucial role here. We are hiring our second Product Designer to own how Pangram shows up visually in its products– across brand, web, and marketing. You will work directly with the founders and our lead designer and product manager to join a design function that is just beginning to take shape.
What You’ll Do
Take ownership of Pangram’s design system, brand, and visual identity
Design and ship web pages for Pangram’s website end-to-end
Create marketing materials and assets across formats: landing pages, social media, launch announcements, campaign visuals, sales collateral, event materials
Contribute to product design (UI, UX, user flows, onboarding) as needed
Run lightweight user studies to inform the design process
Set the quality bar for how Pangram looks and manage QA across the stack
Requirements
2 or more years of experience in product design
A portfolio demonstrating exceptional brand, visual, and graphic design work
Hands on experience shipping real products to users
Design, Art/Creative, and Information Technology
