Your primary focus is shaping the end-to-end user experience for internal tools that support strategic planning and investment decisions. You'll partner closely with product managers, engineers, and business stakeholders to translate complex workflows into intuitive, scalable designs. A typical day might include leading a design review, synthesizing research findings into actionable recommendations, or prototyping solutions for problems no one has solved yet. Beyond the regular beat, you'll influence design direction across adjacent teams and help establish UX standards that raise the bar for the broader organization.
You'll join a small, high-impact design team embedded within an organization of 200+ teammates managing 100+ enterprise technology products. These products enable teams worldwide to plan, deliver, and operate a global network of 5,500+ physical sites across 50 countries. Our UX practice is in its first year. We're building from the ground up: defining how design works, how it scales, and what great looks like. Culture: we move fast, ship iteratively, and let evidence drive decisions. Expect variety, autonomy, and colleagues who care deeply about the people using what we build.
Basic Qualifications
Have an online portfolio or samples of work on resume, demonstrating experience creating great end-to-end, user-centered design solutions and patterns, across desktop and mobile devices
Experience designing and prototyping with tools such as Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, or similar
Experience in delivering design solutions as a UX designer or interaction designer
Knowledge of usability principles and techniques
Preferred Qualifications
Experience acquiring user data (e.g., conducting usability studies, performing user research) and creating personas and journey maps
Experience in mass-market consumer web / mobile products
Experience in visual design expertise demonstrated through mockups and style guides
