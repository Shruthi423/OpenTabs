JOB SUMMARY: This role is responsible for shaping engaging, member-facing content across interactive digital experiences, including games, polls, trivia, and other in-product experiences. The ideal candidate will combine strong UX writing and content design expertise with creative content ideation, crafting clear, on-brand messaging that drives member engagement while collaborating closely with product and engineering teams.
JOB REQUIREMENTS: Write and structure in-product copy, including headlines, prompts, microcopy, CTAs, and results screens.
Develop engaging content concepts, themes, and interactive formats that resonate with members
Partner with product and engineering teams to ensure content and interactions work seamlessly together.
Maintain consistency with established brand voice and content standards across experiences.
Refine messaging and content direction using engagement data and performance insights.
Must-Have Skills: Strong experience in UX writing and content design with a portfolio of in-product content
Proven ability to write concise, engaging, and on-brand microcopy for interactive experiences.
Experience developing content strategies and pitching creative content ideas.  Strong collaboration skills working with product, engineering, and marketing teams
Experience working within established brand guidelines and content systems.
Comfortable using AI tools to accelerate content drafting and ideation.
Consumer Apps, Digital Experiences, Gamification, Interactive Content (Fintech experience is a plus).
