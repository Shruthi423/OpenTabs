Job Description
Parsons is seeking a talented UI/UX Designe r to join our Joint Staff Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO) team. In this role, you will design intuitive, secure, and mission-driven interfaces for data and AI-enabled products, directly supporting Department of Defense operational objectives.
Design and develop user interfaces and user experiences for data, analytics, and AI-enabled platforms supporting Joint Staff missions
Translate mission requirements, user feedback, and technical constraints into wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity visual designs
Collaborate with product managers, technical leads, data scientists, and mission stakeholders to ensure interfaces are user-centric and aligned with operational workflows
Apply industry best practices and DoD standards for usability, accessibility, and security in all design solutions
Conduct user research, usability testing, and iterative design to refine interfaces and maximize user adoption and satisfaction
Develop and maintain design systems, style guides, and reusable UI components for consistent application across products and PODs
Communicate design concepts and rationale to cross-functional teams, advocating for strong user experience throughout the product lifecycle
Support deployment, monitoring, and sustainment of UI/UX solutions within secure DoD environments
Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Computer Science, or related field
Minimum 5 years of experience in UI/UX design for complex applications, preferably in secure or federal environments
Proficiency in design and prototyping tools (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, InVision)
Strong understanding of user-centered design principles, usability, accessibility, and visual design standards
Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver mission-driven products
Excellent communication, presentation, and stakeholder engagement skills
Experience supporting Department of Defense, Joint Staff, or similar government organizations
Familiarity with data visualization, analytics platforms, and AI-enabled product interfaces
Experience designing for secure, cloud-based, or enterprise environments
Knowledge of DoD usability, accessibility, and security standards (e.g., Section 508, NIST)
Advanced certifications in UI/UX, human factors, or design disciplines
Demonstrated ability to lead user research, usability testing, and iterative design in large, matrixed organizations
An active Top Secret SCI security clearance is required for this position.
