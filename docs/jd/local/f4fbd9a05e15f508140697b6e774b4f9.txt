We’re looking for thoughtful, skilled professionals who thrive as trusted partners building technology-agnostic solutions and want to apply their talents supporting customers with difficult and important mission sets.
About The Role
Redhorse transforms the way government uses data and technology. To support this mission, we are seeking a UX/UI Designer who is passionate about building the digital bridge between government agencies and the citizens they serve. In this role, you will lead the charge in transforming manual government processes into intuitive, web-based applications. From developing the initial site concept to implementing complex application interfaces, your work will ensure that essential public services are accessible, responsive, and easy to navigate for everyone.
Support the development of web-based applications and online customer service platforms to modernize government service delivery
Lead the development of site concepts, ensuring a clear vision for the user's digital journey
Design and architect user interfaces that are both aesthetically pleasing and functionally robust
Define the information architecture of websites to ensure intuitive navigation and data flow
Implement high-fidelity interfaces that integrate seamlessly with backend applications
Translate complex mission requirements into responsive design elements and site architectures
Collaborate with developers to ensure the technical feasibility of UI/UX designs
Code and refine application components to ensure design integrity throughout the development lifecycle.
Bachelor’s Degree in Design, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
5+ years of professional experience in UX/UI design and web application development
Demonstrated working knowledge and experience coding in Java
Proven track record of developing site concepts and interface architectures for enterprise-scale websites
Strong portfolio demonstrating experience in transforming complex requirements into user-friendly digital interfaces.
We encourage all candidates who meet the basic requirements to apply, even if you do not have any of the following experience:
Experience with front-end technologies such as HTML and JavaScript
Knowledge of database management systems including Oracle, Sybase, or SQL Server
Familiarity with legacy web technologies such as Active Server Pages (ASP) or Visual Basic
Ability to write and optimize SQL queries for data-driven application interfaces
Experience working in an Agile development environment using tools like Jira and GitLab
Knowledge of Section 508 compliance and accessibility standards for government websites.
