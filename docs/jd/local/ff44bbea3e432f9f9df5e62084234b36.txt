Job description:
Infotainment UI/UX Design Engineer Hyundai America Technical Center, Inc. (HATCI) is looking for an engineer to join the Production Application Team of the Infotainment Software Department, which undertakes various innovation projects related to infotainment and UI (user interface)/UX (user experience) systems software development efforts concerning the next generation of vehicle software architecture for Hyundai, Kia, and Genesis vehicles, as well as the production application of software features and service contents
What You Will Do: Plan and design automotive infotainment features and define engineering specs
Develop user interface, user experience, and wireframes concerning infotainment features
Investigate customer needs and requirements and create technical specs based on investigation results
Design automotive head-unit screens and wireframes of infotainment features by reflecting user needs into head-unit operation specs from a user-centered design perspective
Design infotainment contents and UX for the application of user-friendly and easy-to-understand instructions and button labels for infotainment interfaces
Conduct user acceptance tests and identify improvement items
Work with other software development teams such as Vehicle Control Software, Autonomous Driving Software, Autonomous Development, Electronic Development, and Infotainment Development to support new infotainment feature concept development and POC creation efforts
Collaborate with internal/external project partners, such as content providers, prototyping services, university labs, and tech suppliers, on UI/UX-related items
Conduct competitor system benchmarking, industry technology sensing, and trend analysis on UI/UX-related items
Travel up to 20% of the time, both domestically and internationally
What You Will Bring to the Role: Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in computer engineering, computer science, human-computer interaction, industrial engineering, or a related discipline
1+ years of professional experience in the automotive infotainment field
Understanding of engineering spec definition: SRS, SAD, SDD, and wireframe
Knowledge of graphic interface design principles, particularly concerning design elements
Understanding of human factors and ergonomics for touch-based infotainment interface design efforts
Ability to explain technical topics to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
Excellent time management, self-management, and organization skills
An ability to adapt to shifting priorities, demands, and timelines
Strong written, oral, and interpersonal skills
Good communication skills and an ability to develop/maintain positive relationships with tech experts from the R&D Center and internal counterpart teams
Preferred Qualifications: Master's degree in computer engineering, computer science, human-computer interaction, industrial engineering, or a related discipline
UI/UX developer or engineer experience in HMI application programing, system programing, and/or embedded system development concerning automotive infotainment systems, E/E systems, and/or consumer electronics or relevant products.
Work portfolio that demonstrates design experience with touch-based interface(s) Experience with 12” to 24” LCD screens for car driving contexts is preferred
Experience in production software lifecycle: requirements, system design, development, maintenance, and release readiness for embedded software for vehicle systems
Familiarity with embedded operating systems: Android, Linux, open-source software, QNX, and Yocto
Working knowledge of vehicle communication network systems (e.g., CAN, LIN, Ethernet, etc.) and instrumentation/operation of data acquisition systems, such as ETAS/INCA and Vecto
