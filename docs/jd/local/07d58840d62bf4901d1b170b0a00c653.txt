The User Interface Designer will be responsible for designing and creating intuitive and user-friendly interfaces for web and mobile applications. They will work closely with the development team and stakeholders to gather requirements, create wireframes, and design visually appealing interfaces. The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of user-centered design principles and a passion for creating intuitive and visually appealing interfaces.
· Collaborate with development team and stakeholders to gather requirements and create wireframes
· Design visually appealing interfaces for web and mobile applications
· Ensure interfaces are user-friendly and intuitive
· Stay up to date with the latest design trends and technologies
· Continuously improve the user experience through testing and user feedback
· Translate abstract concepts into simple and elegant user interfaces.
Qualifications:
· 5+ years designing UX/UI for products across the web, mobile or others
· Experience with design software such as Adobe Creative Suite, Adobe XD, Sketch, Figma etc
· Strong understanding of user-centered design principles
· Aware of accessibility WCAG 2.1 AA standards
· Strong communication skills and ability to work in a team environment
· A degree in Graphic Design, Product Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field is preferred.
· Acceptable portfolio submissions are Dribble, Behance, and personal website portfolio
