Location Requirement: Only candidates who are local to New York City will be considered for this position.
Job Description:
Seeking a UX Designer to create engaging, consistent digital experiences across key customer journeys and marketing touchpoints. This role combines visual and interaction design, brand storytelling, usability, and design system thinking.
Design cohesive digital experiences across user journeys and touchpoints.
Create visual and interaction designs aligned with brand standards and design systems.
Translate brand principles into intuitive, user-centered experiences.
Design campaign and marketing experiences that support engagement and business goals.
Develop reusable design patterns and contribute to design system improvements.
Ensure consistency in typography, color, spacing, accessibility, and interactions.
Create production-ready designs with responsive states, interactions, and edge cases.
Partner with Product, Engineering, Marketing, Content, and Design teams for successful implementation.
Manage multiple projects while maintaining quality and consistency.
Communicate design decisions, rationale, and tradeoffs to stakeholders.
Required Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Design, Interaction Design, Communication Design, or related field.
5+ years of experience designing digital products or experiences.
Strong skills in UX, interaction, and visual design.
Experience with brand-driven, campaign, or storytelling experiences.
Experience working with component-based design systems and reusable patterns.
Understanding of content structure, storytelling, and user/business goals.
Strong cross-functional collaboration skills.
Experience with Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) or other component-based CMS platforms.
Proficiency with Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, or similar design tools.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience with Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics, or experimentation tools.
Experience in financial services or other regulated industries.
Experience designing complex, multi-step user journeys.
Ability to work independently in ambiguous and changing environments.
Strong communication, organization, problem-solving, and attention to detail.
