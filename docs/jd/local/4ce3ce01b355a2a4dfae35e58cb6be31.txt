Reporting to SEPTA's Manager of Wayfinding, your work will help lead SEPTA’s digital customer experience transformation, as SEPTA moves to become a best-in-class mobility provider. As SEPTA introduces new rail vehicles, advances the rollout of real-time customer communications, and begins the “Key 2.0” fare payment system modernization, you will design and enhance user interfaces, customer information systems, and digital wayfinding tools that make transit easier to understand and use. While your primary focus will be digital products, you will collaborate closely with colleagues responsible for maps, signage, and environmental graphic design to curate a seamless journey across physical and digital touchpoints. You will be supported by industry-leading colleagues while applying SEPTA's design standards, accessibility requirements, and brand identity across every project.
You'll have the opportunity to learn from customers in stations and on vehicles, conduct user research, prototype and test new digital experiences, and translate real-world rider behavior into thoughtful, data-informed design solutions. Working alongside product managers, developers, communications professionals, and operations staff, you'll see your ideas evolve from concept through implementation, helping improve the daily experience of hundreds of thousands of riders.
Ideal candidates will have 3-5 years of professional experience in UI/UX, product design, interaction design, or digital experience design, with demonstrated expertise in user research, wireframing, prototyping, accessibility, and modern design systems. Experience designing for complex public-facing services, transportation, civic technology, or other large-scale customer experience environments is required.
Leads coordination of projects for service information design content that supports web, mobile app, and digital screens on vehicles and stations.
Collaborates with Information Technology as a Subject Matter Expert on the user interface (UI) design of SEPTA’s website, mobile app, and audio/visual passenger information system (AVPA) screens. Provides high-fidelity prototypes, graphic assets/files, and specifications for front-end development.
Performs quality control/user testing on the website/app before launch for consistency with the SEPTA’s brand, ADA compliance, and User Experience/User Interface (UX/UI) best practices.
Demonstrates leadership in upholding and enforcing all Authority and departmental safety and security policies and procedures, as well as all applicable job safety responsibilities. Immediately reports any safety concerns, compromises or hazards affecting operations, yourself and/or other employees. Responsible for personal safety and champions the safety of others.
