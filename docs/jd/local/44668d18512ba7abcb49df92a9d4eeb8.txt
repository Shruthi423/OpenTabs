Target's Global UX team is a group of design, content, research, and accessibility practitioners and experts, with a passion for improving and innovating our digital experiences for Target's guests, team members, and partners. Here, we pride ourselves on designing tangible, inspiring, and impactful solutions that serve all aspects of our business - and being a part of the team means you will play a critical role in Target's digital experience by increasing ease, simplicity, value, and delight for every touchpoint.
About The Role
As a UX Researcher - AI Enablement, you will contribute research that helps Target understand and improve AI-enabled and agentic experiences for technical and product practitioners and other enterprise team members. This individual-contributor role focuses on scoped projects with immediate impact at the project and team level.
You will work with Lead and Director-level researchers, applying established qualitative and quantitative methods to well-defined questions and contributing to larger programs and pilots. With direction and guidance, you will contribute to research planning, conduct straightforward studies, participate in analysis and synthesis, and communicate findings that support product and experience decisions.
You will also support responsible AI enablement within UX Research by helping test new tools, document effective workflows, and keep research knowledge organized and reusable. You will collaborate with cross-functional partners across UX, Product, Technology, Data Science, Analytics, and the business while building your craft and technical understanding.
Apply established qualitative and quantitative methods to well-defined questions about AI-enabled products, tools, and workflows.
Contribute to research plans, protocols, recruitment, fieldwork, and study logistics with guidance from senior researchers.
Conduct straightforward interviews, usability tests, concept evaluations, and other assigned studies using standard practices.
Support surveys, UX metrics, pilots, benchmarks, and longitudinal research through data collection, quality checks, and basic analysis.
Participate in analysis and synthesis, organize evidence, and communicate clear findings to project partners.
Collaborate with cross-functional partners on research methods, measures, and decisions that affect the immediate work.
Contribute evidence to personas, journey maps, service blueprints, and shared research knowledge.
Help test and document responsible AI-enabled research workflows while meeting project goals, constraints, timelines, and quality standards.
Core responsibilities of this job are described within this job description. Job duties may change at any time due to business needs.
