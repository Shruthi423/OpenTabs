Lead brand and marketing design work across a broad mix of channels, campaigns, and business needs. Create high-quality illustrations, graphics, and other visual assets from concept through final delivery. Build and maintain reusable creative assets such as landing pages, display ads, social media content, marketing decks, presentations, event materials, and other branded collateral. Manage multiple projects independently, prioritize effectively, and deliver high-quality work under tight timelines without day-to-day project management support. Partner with cross-functional teams to develop visuals that connect with creators, Rumble users, advertisers, and business audiences. Help enforce consistency across the organization by applying and championing brand guidelines. Coordinate with external creative partners when additional support or specialized assets are needed. Contribute to other creative and brand initiatives as priorities evolve.
Qualifications
3+ years of professional design experience, with a portfolio that demonstrates strong brand and marketing work across multiple channels and asset types.
3+ years of experience designing email creative, ideally in Figma or a comparable design environment.
Experience producing digital, presentation, and large-format creative assets.
Strong understanding of how to connect brand or marketing strategy to effective visual design.
Proficiency in Figma and strong command of design fundamentals including layout, typography, hierarchy, and composition.
Comfort working in a fast-paced environment with changing priorities, tight deadlines, and multiple concurrent projects.
Highly self-directed, proactive, and organized, with the ability to manage work independently and own outcomes in an environment without dedicated project management support.
General understanding of performance and growth marketing, and how creative work supports campaign outcomes.
Strong collaboration and communication skills, including the ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams and outside creative partners.
Comfort working in Google Workspace and Microsoft Office products, especially for presentation, document, and collaborative asset development.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience designing for technology, media, creator-focused, or advertising-related businesses.
Experience creating social assets for platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.
Agency background or experience supporting multiple brands, campaigns, or audiences in parallel.
Bachelor’s degree in Design, Creative, Visual Communication, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
Experience creating work that supports creator programs, audience growth, advertising, or brand partnerships.
