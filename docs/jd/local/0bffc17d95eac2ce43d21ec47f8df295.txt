The UI UX Designer to support critical enterprise initiatives.
The ideal candidate is a strategic UI/UX professional who goes beyond implementing requirements to understand the underlying business need.
They can translate business objectives into scalable, maintainable designs.
They can confidently assess and enhance vendor-delivered solutions, challenge assumptions constructively, and collaborate with stakeholders to improve the overall application design.
They think beyond individual features to create scalable, intuitive solutions that support long-term business goals.
Primary responsibility is to work on UX and UI design efforts, creating prototypes and wireframes, refining and updating the design system and component library, facilitating user research sessions, and developing product requirements.
Engages with internal and external stakeholders to understand user needs and translates user requirements into highly usable responsive experiences.
