You’ll partner closely with product, engineering, and business stakeholders—and engage directly with customers—to turn insights into thoughtful, validated solutions. As the first dedicated design support for this space, you’ll have a unique opportunity to influence how design works while independently delivering meaningful product improvements.
What you'll do:
Introduce and apply the new Design System to exiting and future experiences, helping drive consistency, scalability, and alignment with enterprise design standards
Conduct a heuristic evaluation of the current experience, identifying usability issues, opportunities for improvement, and recommendations for future enhancements
Support the product roadmap by designing new features and enhancements across the customer journey
Plan and execute customer research and usability testing activities to validate concepts, designs, and product decisions
Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs in Figma
Partner closely with product to define requirements and prioritize opportunities based on customer needs and business goals
Collaborate with engineering teams to ensure designs are feasible and implemented as intended
Participate in design reviews and present recommendations to stakeholders
Product design experience with a strong portfolio showcasing customer-facing digital products and applications
Experience conducting heuristic evaluations, usability testing, and customer research (ideal)
Strong UX and interaction design skills, with the ability to translate complex requirements into intuitive user experiences
Proficiency in Figma, including design systems, component libraries, and prototyping
Ability to work independently and drive projects from discovery through delivery
Banking or financial services experience preferred; experience in other regulated industries (fintech, healthcare, insurance) also welcome
