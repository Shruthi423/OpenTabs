Product Designer (Consultant, Experience & Design)
We are seeking a Product Designer as part of our Consultant, Experience & Design career path to ensure an enterprise cloud data platform is intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs. This opportunity supports a large federal agency's enterprise data modernization initiative focused on building and operating a secure, cloud-native data platform that enables data-driven decision-making across the agency. The Product Designer conducts user research, facilitates discovery sessions, and designs user journeys that improve customer onboarding and platform adoption, translating complex technical capabilities into accessible experiences that help agency teams discover, access, and use enterprise data services effectively. This is a fully remote role.
At Fearless , the consultant ensures that products and services are intuitive, accessible, and meet the needs of diverse users. This role will possess and/or develop the skills to be able to span the domains of Content Design, Product Design, Service Design, Content Strategy, and User Research at a practical application level of proficiency. This Consultant role will work on projects that allow them to implement evidence-based solutions that balance organizational goals with user needs, while fostering inclusive and sustainable design practices across the organization.
Please include a link to your portfolio with your application. Your portfolio helps our hiring team understand your previous work, design approach, and the role you played in creating solutions.
Conduct user research with platform stakeholders to identify pain points, validate assumptions, and inform design decisions.
Design end-to-end user journeys and service workflows that improve customer onboarding and platform usability.
Create wireframes, prototypes, and interactive mockups using Figma to communicate design concepts and validate solutions.
Perform usability testing with end users to evaluate design effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.
Ensure all designs meet federal accessibility standards and support inclusive user experiences.
Collaborate with engineers, product managers, customer success teams, and stakeholders to translate user needs into intuitive platform experiences.
Facilitate discovery workshops to align cross-functional teams around user-centered platform enhancements.
Develop service blueprints and customer experience artifacts that improve platform adoption and service delivery.
Analyze user feedback and usability findings to recommend improvements that enhance customer experience and platform adoption.
Earn the trust of your customers and teammates by delivering world-class services.
Be reliable in completing assigned objectives on time.
Alert your teammates and supervisor immediately when you think a deliverable is at risk.
Gather intelligence and listen to what your client, other people within the customer’s organization and other contractors say about your project, and report back to your Fearless teammates and supervisor (whether it’s good, bad or just informational).
Deliver expected outcomes that are intuitive, accessible, and aesthetically compelling
Offer and implement innovative solutions that include design best practices, tools, and research methods
Serve as a trusted advisor in your area of expertise
