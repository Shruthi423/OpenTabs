Harvey is an AI-native product. As the system reasons, responds, and adapts in real time, the interface must remain consistent and legible. A strong design system ensures that complexity does not leak into the experience. It enables teams to move faster, reduces cognitive load for users, and builds trust in moments where clarity matters most.
What You'll Do
Build, maintain, and evolve production-ready design system components, patterns, documentation, and reusable assets across Figma and emerging AI-native design workflows.
Partner with the Senior Design Systems Designer to scale Harvey's visual language and support the rollout of new system foundations across the product.
Collaborate with product designers and frontend engineers to ensure system components are intuitive to use, accurately implemented, and consistently adopted.
Own and create polished production assets, libraries, prototypes, and documentation that enable teams to build quickly and with confidence.
Support AI-assisted design workflows by preparing components, documentation, and system assets that are optimized for both designers and AI tools.
Maintain the quality and health of the design system through QA, organization, continuous refinement, and operational improvements.
Advocate for exceptional craftsmanship, consistency, and usability across every product surface.
3–5 years of experience as a Product Designer, Production Designer, UI Designer, or Design Systems Designer working on digital products.
Exceptional visual craft with a meticulous eye for detail, layout, spacing, consistency, and production quality.
Exceptional organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple workstreams while maintaining a high quality bar.
Experience building and maintaining component libraries and design systems in Figma, including auto layout, variables, components, and variants.
Curiosity and experience in AI-assisted design tools and evolving implementation workflows, you don't need to be an engineer, but you're excited about where the discipline is heading.
Excellent collaboration and communication skills, with a desire to learn through close partnership with design and engineering.
Comfort working in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment and enthusiasm for helping shape an evolving Design Systems practice.
