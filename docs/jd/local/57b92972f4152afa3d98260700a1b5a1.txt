We’re rebuilding our brand from the ground up. A new identity, bold campaigns, and a world-class digital experience that will support our expansion into new markets and help make US Mobile a household name.
The role
We’re building the creative studio at US Mobile, bringing together designers, filmmakers, photographers and producers to create exceptional work under one roof.
You’ll work across campaigns, web, product marketing, motion, launches and everything in between. You’ll help define how US Mobile looks, feels and communicates at every touchpoint.
We care deeply about craft. We obsess over typography, motion, photography, systems and the smallest details.
Our product is built by leaders, engineers and a product team who care deeply about the details, and our brand should reflect that same mindset.
Our brand is technical and obsessive, our customers don’t need to understand the engineering behind US Mobile but they should never doubt that we do, inside and out.
The best brands feel human and our work should remind people there’s a team of real people behind it that cares deeply about our customers.
Has exceptional taste. You’re constantly looking beyond the obvious, developing a point of view that’s informed by culture, craft and curiosity, and you can explain the thinking behind it
Raises the standard of the work through honest, constructive feedback. You’re politely hardcore, pushing for better while bringing people with you
Has superb visual craft and attention to detail
Sweats typography, layout, composition and motion
Is excited by the idea of helping build a brand people rely on
Wants to build something ambitious alongside a small, highly driven team
If you’re excited by the idea of building work you’re genuinely proud of, alongside people who care just as much as you do, we’d love to hear from you.
