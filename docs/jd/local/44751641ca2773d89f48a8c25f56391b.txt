Our client, a leading organization in the field of virtual and augmented reality technology, is seeking a UX Researcher III to join their team. As a UX Researcher III, you will be part of the Research department supporting innovative development projects. The ideal candidate will have strong analytical skills, excellent communication abilities, and a collaborative mindset, which will align successfully in the organization.
Embed within the prototyping team to conduct rapid, lightweight qualitative research to inform development of AI glasses user experiences.
Participate in demos, design reviews, and working sessions to observe and document user reactions, usability signals, and team feedback.
Design and execute short evaluative sessions, heuristic walkthroughs, and conversational interviews with small participant groups on a quick-turn basis.
Synthesize scattered qualitative feedback into clear, actionable insights delivered within the same week to support ongoing development.
Evaluate how users perceive spatially rendered audio, including localization and externalization, translating perceptual reactions into design guidance.
MA/MS in Speech and Hearing Sciences, Psychoacoustics, Audiology, Cognitive Psychology, Linguistics, Human Computer Interaction, Human Factors, Neuroscience, or a related UX field.
2+ years of experience conducting applied qualitative research on consumer products, especially using interviews, observational studies, and rapid thematic analysis.
Proficiency in conducting qualitative interviews, including conversational and semi-structured formats, with a demonstrated ability to elicit rich insights.
Experience with rapid-cycle research in fast-paced environments where deliverables are needed within days.
Strong interpretive judgment skills to form qualitative insights from limited observations and frame findings to advance team goals.
Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AR/VR technology projects that redefine human perception and communication.
Engagement with a dynamic, innovative team dedicated to creating impactful user experiences.
Hands-on involvement in fast-paced prototyping and iterative development cycles.
Chance to contribute to groundbreaking research in spatial audio and auditory perception.
Work in a collaborative environment that values diverse perspectives and inclusive innovation.
