Inference.ai is building the unified platform for the AI era — bringing models, agents, and GPU compute together in one stack so teams can deploy AI without the operational overhead. Our products span one-click agent deployment, intelligent model routing that cuts inference costs by up to 75%, and enterprise-scale GPU infrastructure, all accessed through a single API. We're a fast-moving team solving hard infrastructure problems at the center of the AI buildout, and we're looking for people who want to help shape that stack from the ground up.
About the Role
We are seeking a UX/UI Intern to support both product design and brand execution across the company. This role is ideal for someone who is excited to work across multiple surfaces, from improving core product interfaces to creating visual assets for marketing and community engagement.
You will play a key role in helping us elevate the overall quality, consistency, and usability of everything we ship. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to refine and implement user-centric solutions for various projects.
What You'll Do
Improve and refine UI across our platform (e.g., GPU calculator, dashboards, and web experiences)
Design intuitive product interfaces that simplify complex technical concepts
Create visual assets for social media, announcements, and marketing campaigns
Design branded materials such as swag, event assets, and promotional content
Contribute to the development and consistency of our visual identity
Collaborate closely with engineers and founders to quickly bring ideas to life
Qualifications
Hands-on experience in creating Mockups, Visual Design, and Prototyping
Strong skills in User Experience Design (UED), ensuring a user-centric approach
Familiarity with Front-End Development to translate designs into functional interfaces
Excellent problem-solving abilities and attention to detail
Ability to collaborate effectively in team settings
Relevant coursework or prior experience in UX/UI design is a plus
