Primary Skills:  Figma (advanced), Prototyping (advanced), Interaction Design (advanced), Systems Design (intermediate), Visual Design (advanced)
This role focuses on creating intuitive and efficient user experiences for professional tax accountants, translating complex workflows into clear interfaces. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to define problems, explore solutions, and iterate on designs based on user feedback. The position requires a strong understanding of design systems and the ability to de-risk design decisions through prototyping and testing.
Key Responsibilities: Design user journeys from concept to final execution for tax professional software.
Simplify complex data-heavy tax and accounting workflows into user-friendly interfaces.
Partner with product, engineering, and research to solve design challenges.
Contribute to and maintain a consistent design system across products.
Utilize prototyping and testing to validate design choices before development.
Must-Have Skills: Proven experience designing complex B2B software.
Demonstrated portfolio showcasing end-to-end interaction and systems design.
Comfort designing for power users with dense data and complex task flows.
Industry Experience: Experience in the financial technology or accounting software domain is preferred for this role.
