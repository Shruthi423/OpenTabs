You are also AI-native in how you work. You use AI tools to accelerate research, synthesis, concept development, prototyping, content design, design exploration, and critique while maintaining ownership of the quality and coherence of the final experience.
You are energized by small teams, high autonomy, direct customer exposure, and the opportunity to help define how a new product design organization should operate.
What You Will Do:
Shape the product from the beginning. Work with product managers and engineers from the earliest stages of discovery to define the customer problem, product thesis, and experience strategy. Help the team decide what to build, not simply how it should look after the decision has been made. Turn ambiguity into clear concepts, flows, prototypes, and product direction.
Design AI-native experiences. Define how users interact with agents, models, tools, and automated workflows. Design for intent, delegation, oversight, uncertainty, transparency, correction, escalation, and control. Create experiences that help users understand what the system is doing, when they should trust it, and when they need to intervene.
Simplify complex workflows. Develop a deep understanding of users, industry processes, data, constraints, and business goals. Reduce complicated enterprise workflows into experiences that feel coherent, intuitive, and purposeful without removing the controls expert users need.
Prototype to learn. Create prototypes early and often to explore product ideas, test interaction models, and learn directly from customers and design partners. Use working prototypes rather than static presentations wherever possible. Move fluidly from rough concepts to detailed production-ready designs.
Own the end-to-end experience. Design across the full customer journey, including onboarding, core workflows, agent interactions, configuration, collaboration, approvals, exceptions, errors, and ongoing use. Ensure the product works as a coherent system rather than a collection of individual screens.
Set the quality bar. Hold a high standard for interaction design, visual design, content, information architecture, accessibility, and product coherence. Review the product continuously and identify weak flows, unclear language, poor defaults, inconsistent behavior, unnecessary complexity, and low-quality details.
Work closely with engineering. Partner directly with engineers throughout implementation, not only at handoff. Understand technical constraints and possibilities, make thoughtful tradeoffs, and stay engaged through production. Use coded prototypes and AI-assisted development tools where they improve the speed or fidelity of the work.
Learn from customers and product behavior. Work directly with customers, design partners, domain experts, and internal teams to understand needs and observe how the product is used. Combine qualitative research, product data, evaluation results, and support signals to improve the experience continuously.
Operate AI-native. Use AI tools as a core part of your daily design workflow. Apply them to research synthesis, concept generation, prototyping, content exploration, design systems, critique, and documentation. Build reusable workflows and context that increase your own leverage and the effectiveness of the broader product team.
Raise the design bar. Help the team distinguish between functional design and exceptional product design. Provide clear, constructive critique and help other designers strengthen their product judgment, craft, and AI-native practices. Contribute to the shared design principles, systems, tools, and operating model of the broader product organization.
