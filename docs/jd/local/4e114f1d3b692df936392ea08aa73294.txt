We’re looking for a UX Design System Designer to join a collaborative UX team focused on building, maintaining, and scaling an enterprise Design System. This role is ideal for someone who loves creating reusable design patterns, improving user experiences, and working closely with Product and Engineering teams.
You’ll also have the opportunity to explore how AI can improve design workflows, documentation, and the connection between design and development.
What You’ll Do
Own and help evolve the UX Design System, including components, patterns, and guidelines
Create wireframes, mockups, prototypes, user flows, and high-fidelity designs
Use Figma to create scalable and reusable design solutions
Partner with Product Owners, Engineers, and UX team members from concept through implementation
Work in an Agile/Scrum environment to define user stories and UX deliverables
Collaborate with developers to make sure designs are practical and easy to implement
Advocate for the user and use UX best practices, research, and business requirements to influence product decisions
Build and maintain design system documentation and usage guidelines
Help create consistent experiences across digital products and platforms
Conduct usability testing, user interviews, and other UX research as needed
Gather feedback from users and stakeholders and use it to improve designs
Present design concepts and recommendations to technical and non-technical stakeholders
Manage multiple projects and communicate timelines, risks, and project status
Look for opportunities to improve and standardize design workflows
Explore AI-enabled design workflows, including Figma AI, automation, design-to-code, and prompt-based tools
Help develop AI-ready design practices that improve consistency, documentation, and delivery speed
Strong experience working with Design Systems
Experience creating and maintaining component libraries, reusable patterns, and design guidelines
Experience working closely with developers throughout the design and implementation process
Strong understanding of UX, interaction design, and user-centered design principles
Experience with responsive, adaptive, and accessible design
Understanding of qualitative and quantitative UX research methods
Experience with usability testing and user interviews
Ability to organize complex information into clear user flows, wireframes, and design solutions
Ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams and stakeholders
Experience quickly iterating designs based on feedback and user testing
