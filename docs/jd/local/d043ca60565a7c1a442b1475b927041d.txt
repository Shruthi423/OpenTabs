The Junior Mobile Game UI/UX Designer will collaborate with designers, artists, and developers to create intuitive, engaging interfaces for mobile games, including menus, HUDs, onboarding flows, and in-game interactions. You will contribute to user flows, wireframes, and prototypes, refine layouts and visual hierarchies, and ensure consistency with the game’s art direction and design systems. Day-to-day tasks include gathering requirements from the team, translating game design concepts into user-friendly experiences, preparing assets for implementation, and iterating based on playtesting and feedback. You will also help document UI/UX guidelines and support the improvement of usability and accessibility across game features.
Qualifications
Foundational UI/UX design skills for mobile games, including user flows, wireframes, interaction design, and basic prototyping.
Ability to create clean, readable visual designs for game interfaces, including layouts, iconography, and typography that support gameplay.
Experience collaborating with game designers and developers, receiving and integrating feedback, and iterating on design solutions.
Proficiency with common design tools and familiarity with mobile platform guidelines.
Understanding of user-centered design principles, usability, and accessibility in interactive entertainment experiences.
Portfolio demonstrating UI/UX work for games or interactive applications; personal or academic projects are welcome.
Interest in playful, experimental projects beyond traditional games and enthusiasm for learning and growing in a junior design role.
Relevant education or training in design, human-computer interaction, game design, or a related field is beneficial but not required.
