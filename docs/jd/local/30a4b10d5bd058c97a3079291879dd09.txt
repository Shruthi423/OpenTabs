We at Coforge are hiring a UX Researcher with the following skillset:
Partner with Product Owners, Designers, and Stakeholders to deeply understand business challenges and advocate for exceptional user experiences.

Support and expand UX Research Operations (ResearchOps), including research infrastructure, participant recruitment, and research governance.
Mentor designers and junior researchers while fostering a culture of user-centered design.

Strong presentation, communication, stakeholder management, and project management skills.
Advanced Excel or equivalent data analysis capabilities.
