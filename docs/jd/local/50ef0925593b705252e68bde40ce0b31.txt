About The Role
The role owns the end-to-end design lifecycle, translating complex user needs and technical constraints into intuitive, human-centered digital experiences.
You will collaborate closely with product managers, UX researchers, and software engineers to shape product direction and build polished interfaces used by millions.
Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing to uncover actionable insights that inform product strategy and interface design
Create low-fidelity wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interactive prototypes using Figma for cross-platform applications
Establish, scale, and maintain a comprehensive design system, ensuring consistent patterns and accessible UI components
Partner with front-end engineers to ensure faithful implementation of design specs, accessibility standards, and micro-interactions
Facilitate design sprints and stakeholder workshops to align product vision with user needs and business metrics
3–6 years of experience in product design, UX/UI design, or interaction design within a tech-driven environment
Expert-level proficiency in Figma and modern prototyping tools, with a strong portfolio showcasing complex problem-solving and visual polish
Solid understanding of WCAG accessibility guidelines, responsive design principles, and modern front-end technologies (HTML/CSS)
Experience translating quantitative data and qualitative research into iterative design improvements
Bonus: Experience designing for enterprise SaaS platforms or designing AI-driven conversational interfaces
